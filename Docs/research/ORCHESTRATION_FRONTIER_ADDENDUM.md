# Orchestration Frontier Addendum (beyond-SOTA)

Source: orchestration-frontier-beyond workflow. Fuse with ORCHESTRATION_SPEC.md.

## Thesis
Best-in-world autonomous-estate intelligence is not a flow-level switch that flips from "gated" to "auto" and spawns view-tabs. It is a closed-loop, self-improving estate brain whose autonomy is a CONTINUOUS, EARNED, LIVE-RE-EVALUATED function — and whose every autonomous act is rehearsed against a calibrated world-model BEFORE it touches reality and attested AFTER. Four shifts separate frontier from the brief. (1) Autonomy is computed per-DECISION, not declared per-flow: a decision-theoretic Value-of-Information gate asks the owner only the single question whose expected utility exceeds the cost of interrupting; a 2-D reversibility×blast-radius surface (not Borjie's current flat 5-tier risk-tier.ts) frees the cheap reversible body of a flow while gating only its one irreversible step; Write-Staging shrinks even that by turning every LedgerService.post() into a promotable pending draft; and conformal calibration makes "90% sure" actually true 90% of the time before confidence is allowed to drive delegation. (2) Trust is EARNED and revocable, not granted: a cryptographically-signed Delegation Mandate with composition-aware escalation ("20 individually-allowed wires that sum to an unauthorized transfer") replaces the stateless toggle, autonomy is promoted per task-class only when the lower confidence bound on the audited success rate clears threshold, and a dynamic re-gating engine auto-demotes "auto" the moment the world shifts (novel counterparty, FX past the 27-Mar USD-cliff regime, drift toward a sovereign/kill_switch prefix, a defection-probe hit on internal activations). (3) The brain ANTICIPATES instead of reacting: simulate-before-act is an ARCHITECTURALLY FORCED pre-commit stage (agents provably invoke world-models <1% of the time when it is merely optional), running the action forward against an enterprise digital twin with generative-agent counterparties — including the null action ("file nothing → Licence #4471 lapses 14-Aug → pit dark") — and a critic vetoes outcomes outside policy; WALL-E-style rule-learning keeps that twin honest against the realized ledger so foresight stays calibrated and auditable. (4) The whole compound system IMPROVES itself inside the governor: the master-brain→lens-router→juniors→synthesizer→Auditor graph is a learnable object — GEPA/TextGrad backpropagate the Auditor's natural-language rejection verdicts (which the brief discards after gating) as a richer-than-scalar gradient; a DGM/ADAS meta-MD writes NEW juniors as code when the estate hits a novel recurring problem; AFlow/MASS learn the workflow graph and team topology per problem-class; a Voyager automatic curriculum drills the estate's actual blind-spots on replayed/synthetic data in the existing reflexion-sleep-canary window — but ONLY in the mutable layer (juniors/prompts/workflows/topology), empirically gated against the Auditor on replayed cases, in-sandbox, NEVER touching the inviolable governor (policy-gate, kill-switch, hash-chained audit, money/licence/deletion stay dual-control HITL forever). Crucially, Borjie was BORN with the moat every RSI paper bolts on as an afterthought (isolated-vm + FORCE-RLS + hash-chained audit + fail-closed kill-switch + four-eye + mandatory evidence-required Auditor), and my codebase inspection shows the frontier scaffolds already physically exist — conformal-calibration-online, ledger-attestor (merkle + external WORM sink, Rekor-ready), bi-temporal knowledge-graph, causal-inference/counterfactual, prompt-evolution/gepa-optimizer, skill-library/voyager-library/compile-from-traces, junior-evolution-worker, dp-federation, kernel/counter-model + critics — so best-in-world here is overwhelmingly a WIRING-AND-EXTENSION program, not greenfield: turn the offense moat ON, fuse it to the defense moat as ONE system (every self-improving memory write gets the same shield/eval/attestation as an action, because a poisoned memory re-infects forever), and make AUTO mean "runs inside a proven, calibrated, re-gated, attested box" — categorically more trustworthy than a tired human rubber-stamping a green light.

## Upgrades (beyond the brief)
### Per-decision Value-of-Information ask-vs-act gate (replace the flow-label switch)  [transformational / now]
- adds: The brief interrupts on a static FLOW label ('this flow is gated'). This computes, per DECISION, the expected-utility gain of asking vs the cognitive cost of interrupting the owner, and surfaces only the single highest-information question (Bayesian experimental design). Turns 'should I spawn a tab / ask the owner?' from a 1-bit flag into a utility calculation — the switch can only ask-about-everything or ask-about-nothing; VoI asks about the ONE thing worth the owner's attention right now. Directly upgrades the tab-need-detector scoring-matrix, which today has no VoI / expected-utility / cognitive-cost term.
- areas: packages/tab-need-detector (scoring-matrix.ts, proposal-emitter.ts), packages/autonomy-governance/src/caps (cap-evaluator.ts), packages/central-intelligence/src/kernel/proactive-nudge.ts, packages/central-intelligence/src/kernel/uncertainty-policy.ts, packages/notifications (EFE/VoI-ranked sink, not threshold-ranked)
### 2-D reversibility×blast-radius autonomy surface + Write-Staging + Irreversibility Budget  [transformational / near]
- adds: The switch gates a whole flow at its most-dangerous step, freezing all the cheap reversible steps too. Borjie's risk-tier.ts is a FLAT 5-tier ladder (read→mutate→destroy→billing→external-comm) — it conflates reversibility with blast-radius. This makes it a 2-D surface so the reversible body of a gated flow runs free and only the one irreversible step gates; Write-Staging then interposes a draft/pending layer that CONVERTS an irreversible L5 LedgerService.post() into a reversible L4 pending ledger draft the owner promotes; an Irreversibility Budget caps cumulative irreversible consequence before forced re-authorization (an economic anti-runaway invariant mirroring the kill-switch rule). The switch has no concept of a cumulative irreversible-action cap.
- areas: packages/central-intelligence/src/kernel/risk-tier.ts (flat→2-D), services/payments-ledger (pending-draft/promotion layer around LedgerService.post()), packages/autonomy-governance/src/caps (irreversibility budget in rolling state), packages/central-intelligence/src/kernel/four-eye-approval.ts
### Confidence×consequence×reversibility-adaptive delegation gated on conformal calibration  [transformational / now]

A high-confidence-CALIBRATED-reversible act runs auto even inside a gated flow; a low-confidence-irreversible act gates even in an auto flow. The conformal-calibration-online package (aci.ts) already exists but is not wired into the autonomy cap-evaluator, which currently takes neither confidence nor reversibility as inputs.

## Rejected (intellectual honesty)
- Godel-Agent-style runtime self-modification of the OUTER loop (the kernel rewriting its own orchestration logic at runtime guided only by high-level objectives). Most radical of the self-improving frontier, but it dissolves the fixed outer loop and is the RSI hazard surface (reward-hacking, model-collapse, latent backdoor amplification). Frontier-correct posture: borrow its expressiveness ONLY in the mutable layer (juniors/prompts/workflows/topology) under the DGM empirical-fitness gate + isolated-vm sandbox, and FORBID any self-edit to the inviolable governor (policy-gate, kill-switch, hash-chained audit, RLS, four-eye). Self-modify capability, never policy. Reject the unguarded form outright.
- Fully autonomous LedgerService.post() / licence suspension / record deletion via earned trust. Even a statistically-promoted, calibrated, twin-rehearsed, attested AUTO act should NOT remove dual-control HITL on money-movement, licence status, or deletion. CLAUDE.md makes these the agent-immutable constitution; the entire RSI-safety literature conditions its promise on exactly this. AUTO here means 'fully drafted, simulated, vetoed, and queued for one-click four-eye promotion' — never silent execution. Write-Staging makes that ergonomic without crossing the line.
- Naive internal junior marketplace with auction clearing and no regulator (the literal Magentic-Marketplace setup). Proven to be exploited: cheap-confident juniors win bids they can't fulfill, sophisticated agents exploit weaker ones, pricing never converges among unequal-capability agents. Only the HYBRID is recommended (reserve-quality floor + reputation weight + Auditor-as-regulator). Reject the pure market.
- DSPy/MIPROv2 and TextGrad as the PRIMARY prompt-optimization engine. The underlying papers are UNVERIFIED in the brief, and Borjie already ships a verified GEPA optimizer (prompt-evolution/gepa-optimizer.ts) which beats MIPROv2 >10% at far fewer rollouts and uses the Auditor's natural-language verdicts Borjie already emits. Adopt GEPA now; keep TextGrad as the longer-horizon end-to-end backprop substrate, but don't lead with the unverified mechanism.
- Full generative-agent counterparty simulation (every buyer/regulator/competitor as an autonomous LLM agent, EconAgent-scale) as a near-term build. The enterprise digital twin is recommended, but starting with FULL generative-agent counterparties is over-engineering and a calibration risk (confident-wrong foresight is worse than none). Start with a deterministic/discrete-event twin of the estate's own state machine (ledger, licences, FX, royalties) under WALL-E rule-correction; add generative counterparties only after the twin is proven calibrated against the realized ledger.
- Auditing every AUTO action by the Auditor at O(volume). The principal-agent / reputation literature shows this is the wrong cost curve. Recommend risk-proportional audit SAMPLING (reputation-weighted bids + anomaly-triggered audits) so Auditor cost scales with RISK, not VOLUME — but reject the naive 'Auditor checks everything' default the current architecture implies, on cost grounds at real estate decision volume.
- On-chain constitutional governance (AgentCity-style blockchain constitutional review). The constitution-as-code idea is recommended (CLAUDE.md hard rules as the agent-immutable tier enforced by the kernel, separation of powers via four-eye), but the ON-CHAIN substrate is UNVERIFIED, adds operational/regulatory complexity inappropriate for a Tanzania-first artisanal-mining estate, and Borjie's hash-chain + external Merkle anchor already delivers the tamper-evidence the chain would provide. Adopt governance-as-code; reject the blockchain substrate.

## Verdict
