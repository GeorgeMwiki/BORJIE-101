# Cross-Cutting + Adjacent Domain Map — Mining Estate ∩ Real Estate

**Last updated:** 2026-06-08
**Audience:** Borjie engineers building Mr. Mwikila's autonomous-MD
capability surface; junior-agent designers; auditors validating the
evidence/standards chain behind AI recommendations.
**Purpose:** Enumerate the COMPLETE map of cross-cutting and adjacent
professional domains that an autonomous Managing Director must master to
run **either** a mining estate **or** a real-estate estate — i.e. the
domains shared by both mandates. For each domain: sub-domains, the real
standards/codes/regulators/professional bodies, at least one real cited
URL (UNVERIFIED where not directly fetched), and why it serves BOTH a
mining-estate and a real-estate operating system.

> **How to read this.** A mining estate and a real-estate estate differ
> in their *core* domain (orebody geology + extraction vs. building +
> tenancy), but they share an enormous *general management* spine: both
> are capital-intensive, land-based, regulated, multi-asset, multi-
> entity businesses that must finance, build, staff, insure, govern,
> disclose, transact, and hand down across generations. This dossier
> maps that shared spine. The mining-only / real-estate-only deep codes
> (JORC vs RICS Red Book, GISTM vs building codes) are flagged where a
> domain has parallel-but-distinct standards on each side.

## 1. Corporate Finance + Accounting (IFRS) + Tax + Treasury

**Sub-domains:** financial accounting + consolidation; management
accounting + cost engineering; project / structured finance + capital
budgeting (NPV/IRR/payback); audit + assurance; corporate tax + indirect
tax (VAT/GST); transfer pricing; royalty + resource taxation; treasury +
cash management; FX + interest-rate hedging; debt + capital markets;
lease accounting; revenue recognition; impairment.

**Standards / bodies:**
- **IFRS / IAS** — issued by the **IASB** (IFRS Foundation). Core for
  both: IFRS 15 (revenue), IFRS 16 (leases — added ~$3T of lease
  obligations to balance sheets, critical for both mine-equipment and
  property leases), IFRS 9 (financial instruments / hedging), IAS 36
  (impairment), IFRS 3 (business combinations). IFRS 6 (Exploration for
  & Evaluation of Mineral Resources) is the mining-specific arm;
  investment-property IAS 40 is the real-estate-specific arm.
- **IFRS 3 Business Combinations** — acquisition method, fair-value
  measurement of assets/liabilities at acquisition date (M&A overlap).
- **Auditing:** ISA (International Standards on Auditing) — **IAASB**.
- **Professional bodies:** ACCA, ICAEW, AICPA, IMA, CIMA (management
  accounting), CFA Institute (investment/valuation analysis).
- **Treasury:** **ISO 20022** payments messaging (SWIFT/CHAPS/TARGET2
  migration through 2024-25); **Association of Corporate Treasurers
  (ACT)** body of knowledge; IFRS 9 hedge accounting for FX/rates.

**Real URLs:**
- https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-16-leases/
- https://www.ifrs.org/news-and-events/updates/iasb/2026/iasb-update-march-2026/
- https://www.treasurers.org/hub/treasurer-magazine/what-every-treasurer-must-know-about-ifrs-9
- https://www.treasurers.org/best-practice/iso-20022-resource-hub
- ICAEW IFRS 16: https://www.icaew.com/technical/corporate-reporting/ifrs/ifrs-accounting-standards-tracker/ifrs-16-leases

**Why it serves both:** Mining and real estate are both capital-
intensive, multi-entity, multi-currency, audited businesses. Both raise
project/structured finance, capitalize long-lived assets, run lease
portfolios (equipment vs. property), hedge FX (TZS/USD exposure for
Borjie), and must produce IFRS-compliant consolidated statements and
manage group treasury. The MD must read a balance sheet, model a DCF,
and manage liquidity identically across both estate types.

## 2. Law + Contracts + Compliance + Regulatory

**Sub-domains:** land tenure + licensing (mining licences/leases vs land
title/leasehold); contract drafting + administration; construction /
EPC contracts; AML/KYC + beneficial-ownership; sanctions + export
controls; anti-bribery/anti-corruption (FCPA/UKBA); competition/anti-
trust; corporate/company law; dispute resolution + arbitration;
regulatory affairs + permitting; data-protection law (see #9); employment
law (see #6); environmental law (see #3/#16).

**Standards / bodies:**
- **FIDIC** suite (Red/Yellow/Silver Books) — global construction/EPC
  contract standard used for both mine builds and property development.
- **ICC** + **UNCITRAL** arbitration rules; **LCIA**; ICSID for
  investor-state.
- **US FCPA** + **UK Bribery Act 2010** (failure-to-prevent offence;
  unlimited fines, up to 10 yrs). **ISO 37001** (anti-bribery MS, 2025
  ed.) + **ISO 37301** (compliance MS) — aligned to DOJ FCPA Guide, UK
  MoJ adequate-procedures, OECD good-practice.
- **FATF** Recommendations (AML/CFT); beneficial-ownership registers.
- **UN Guiding Principles on Business & Human Rights (UNGP)**; OECD
  Guidelines for MNEs.
- Sector regulators: mining ministries / mining commissions (e.g.
  Tanzania Mining Commission) vs land/housing authorities + RERA-style
  property regulators.

**Real URLs:**
- ISO 37001:2025: https://www.iso.org/standard/37001
- FCPA vs UKBA: https://abacgroup.com/fcpa-and-uk-bribery-act/
- FIDIC (org): https://fidic.org/ — UNVERIFIED (not fetched)
- UNGP (OHCHR): https://www.ohchr.org/en/business — UNVERIFIED

**Why it serves both:** Both estates are land-tenure + licence + contract
businesses operating in corruption-exposed jurisdictions. The MD must
administer FIDIC-style construction contracts, run KYC/AML on
counterparties (buyers, tenants, JV partners), maintain anti-bribery
controls when dealing with licensing/permitting authorities, and resolve
disputes via arbitration. The legal-compliance engine is near-identical;
only the sector regulator differs.

## 3. ESG + Climate Disclosure (ISSB/TCFD/GRI) + Carbon

**Sub-domains:** sustainability-related financial disclosure; climate
risk + scenario analysis; GHG accounting (Scope 1/2/3); net-zero target-
setting; nature/biodiversity (TNFD); water stewardship; tailings/waste
(mining) vs embodied-carbon/green-building (real estate); social impact
+ community; double materiality; assurance of sustainability reports.

**Standards / bodies:**
- **ISSB** (within IFRS Foundation): **IFRS S1** (general sustainability
  disclosure) + **IFRS S2** (climate) — built on the four TCFD pillars
  (Governance, Strategy, Risk Management, Metrics & Targets); incorporate
  **SASB** industry metrics; ~21 jurisdictions adopting as of 1 Jan 2026.
- **TCFD** — recommendations fully incorporated into ISSB.
- **GRI Standards** — multi-stakeholder/impact materiality; ISSB designed
  for compatibility.
- **GHG Protocol** (WRI + WBCSD) — Corporate Standard, Scope 2 Guidance,
  Scope 3 Standard, Product Standard. Underpins CDP, ISSB, CSRD, CA SB253.
- **SBTi** Corporate Net-Zero Standard (Criteria v1.3.1, Apr 2026): near-
  term targets include 67% of Scope 3, long-term 90%.
- **EU CSRD / ESRS**; **TNFD** (nature); **CDP** (disclosure platform).

**Real URLs:**
- https://www.ifrs.org/sustainability/knowledge-hub/introduction-to-issb-and-ifrs-sustainability-disclosure-standards/
- https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/ifrs-sustainability-standards-navigator/ifrs-s2-climate-related-disclosures/
- https://www.ifrs.org/sustainability/tcfd/
- https://ghgprotocol.org/standards
- https://files.sciencebasedtargets.org/production/files/Net-Zero-Standard-Criteria.pdf

**Why it serves both:** Investors, lenders, and regulators now demand
ISSB/TCFD-aligned, GHG-Protocol-grounded disclosure from any capital-
intensive land-using enterprise. Mining's footprint (tailings, energy,
land disturbance) and real estate's footprint (operational + embodied
carbon, ~40% of global emissions) both flow through the SAME disclosure
machinery: governance → strategy → risk → metrics/targets, with Scope
1/2/3 accounting and net-zero pathways. The MD runs one ESG engine.

## 4. Project Management

**Sub-domains:** scope/schedule/cost (triple constraint); earned-value
management; capital-project / megaproject delivery; portfolio + program
management; lean/agile + stage-gate; commissioning/handover; benefits
realization; contract + claims management (links #2).

**Standards / bodies:**
- **PMI PMBOK Guide** (7th ed., principles-based) + **PMP** credential.
- **PRINCE2** (AXELOS) — process-driven methodology.
- **ISO 21500 / 21502** (project mgmt guidance) + **ISO 21503/21504**
  (program/portfolio); **ISO 10006** (quality in projects). ISO 21500
  overlaps PMBOK >90%.
- **AACE International** (cost engineering, TCM framework, estimating
  classes) — heavily used in mining/construction capital projects.
- **APM** (Association for Project Management) Body of Knowledge.

**Real URLs:**
- https://cpmp.us/pmbok-guide-and-standards-for-project-management/
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_21500
- AACE: https://web.aacei.org/ — UNVERIFIED (not fetched)

**Why it serves both:** Both estates deliver large, multi-year capital
projects — a new mine/processing plant + camp vs. a development/
construction/fit-out program. Both need EVM, stage-gates, cost
engineering (AACE), schedule control, and claims management on FIDIC
contracts. The same PM discipline governs "build the asset" on both sides.

## 7. Risk Management + Insurance + Internal Controls (SOX/COSO)

**Sub-domains:** enterprise risk management (ERM); internal control over
financial reporting (ICFR / SOX 404); internal audit; fraud risk;
business continuity + crisis management; insurance + risk transfer
(property/casualty, business interruption, environmental, D&O, political
risk, tailings/dam liability); risk assessment techniques; controls
testing + GRC tooling.

**Standards / bodies:**
- **COSO** — Internal Control–Integrated Framework (ICIF 2013: 5
  components, 17 principles, anchors SOX 404) + ERM Framework (2017).
  Recent supplements: sustainability (ICSR 2023), RPA (2024), internal
  control over generative AI (Feb 2026). Sponsored by AICPA, IMA, AAA,
  IIA, FEI.
- **SOX §404** (US) — ICFR attestation.
- **ISO 31000** (risk management — principles + framework) + **ISO
  31010** (risk assessment techniques) + **ISO 22301** (business
  continuity).
- **IIA** International Professional Practices Framework (internal audit)
  + **Global Internal Audit Standards (2024)**.

**Real URLs:**
- https://www.coso.org/guidance-on-ic
- COSO vs SOX: https://auditboard.com/blog/difference-between-coso-and-sox
- ISO 31000 (org page): https://www.iso.org/iso-31000-risk-management.html (UNVERIFIED — returned HTTP 403 on fetch; standard confirmed via secondary sources)

**Why it serves both:** Both estates face catastrophic physical risk
(tailings-dam failure / mine collapse vs structural failure / fire) +
financial/control risk. Both run ERM (ISO 31000 / COSO ERM), maintain
SOX-grade internal controls if listed/financed, place complex insurance
towers (BI, environmental, D&O, political risk), and rely on internal
audit. The control + insurance + ERM stack is identical in shape.

## 11. Family Office + Succession + Governance

**Sub-domains:** family governance (family constitution/charter, family
council, advisory board); ownership + entity structuring (trusts, SPVs,
holdcos, cross-border); succession + continuity planning; estate + tax
planning; next-gen education + readiness; philanthropy governance;
investment policy + wealth diversification; conflict/dispute resolution;
beneficial-ownership compliance (links #2).

**Standards / bodies:**
- **STEP** (Society of Trust and Estate Practitioners) — qualifications
  + standards for trusts/estates.
- **OECD / G20 Principles of Corporate Governance**; ICGN; national
  codes (e.g. King IV in South Africa) — bridge to family-firm boards.
- Practitioner best-practice (Goldman Sachs PWM, TIGER 21, Cresset,
  Creative Planning): three governance layers = documents (constitution/
  charter/shareholder agreements) + bodies (family council/advisory/exec
  committees) + processes (meeting rhythms, voting, delegated authority).
- Empirical: only ~⅓ of wealthy families retain wealth into the next
  generation — failures are governance/systems, not returns.

**Real URLs:**
- https://pwm.gs.com/nam/en-us/insights/wealth-planning/family-governance/key-considerations-for-working-with-a-family-office
- https://tiger21.com/insights/setting-up-a-family-office-a-guide-for-uhnwis/
- STEP: https://www.step.org/ — UNVERIFIED

**Why it serves both:** Borjie's owner is a family-estate owner (Mr.
Mwikila explicitly covers "family office, succession, succession, and
the full asset register"). Whether the underlying assets are mines or
buildings, the family-governance, trust/SPV structuring, succession,
estate-tax, and next-gen-readiness disciplines are identical. The MD
must run the family-office layer above the operating estate either way.

property estate's buildings + plant + FM crews are managed with the SAME

plan/cut-off-grade/blend optimization + commodity-price forecasting vs
space/lease optimization + occupancy/rent forecasting — using the SAME
OR toolkit (LP/MILP, simulation, S&OP, revenue management, ML
forecasting). Decision science is the quantitative core of the MD's
planning brain regardless of estate type.

## 15. Valuation (cross-cutting professional discipline)

**Sub-domains:** asset/business valuation; market/income/cost
approaches; DCF + comparables; impairment testing (links #1); collateral
+ financing valuation; portfolio/NAV valuation; competent/qualified-
person sign-off.

**Standards / bodies (shared spine + parallel arms):**
- **IVS** (International Valuation Standards) — **IVSC** — the common
  global valuation spine; IVS Exposure Draft consultation 2026 → RICS
  Red Book 2028 to follow.
- **Real-estate arm:** **RICS Red Book Global** (incorporates IVS;
  effective 31 Jan 2025; ESG + tech/data guidance added).
- **Mining arm:** **CRIRSCO** family of mineral-reporting codes —
  **JORC** (Australasia), **SAMREC** (S. Africa), **NI 43-101 / CIM**
  (Canada), **SME Guide** (USA), **PERC** (Europe), Chile Certification
  Code; plus mineral-asset *valuation* codes **VALMIN** (AU), **CIMVAL**
  (CA), **SAMVAL** (ZA).
- Business valuation: CFA Institute, ASA, IVSC professional standards.

**Real URLs:**
- https://www.rics.org/profession-standards/rics-standards-and-guidance/sector-standards/valuation-standards/red-book/international-valuation-standards
- https://crirsco.com/
- https://www.jorc.org/docs/JORC_code_2012.pdf
- https://www.samcode.co.za/samcode-ssc/samrec

**Why it serves both:** Every estate decision — financing, M&A,
impairment, collateral, insurance, succession — rests on credentialed
valuation. Both estates sit on the SAME IVS spine; they differ only in
the specialist arm (RICS Red Book for property vs CRIRSCO/JORC + VALMIN/
CIMVAL/SAMVAL for mineral assets). The MD must commission, read, and
challenge standards-based valuations on both sides.

management-system spine for quality, environment, and safety, integrated
via Annex SL. This is the operating-discipline backbone beneath ESG (#3)
and HSE (#17) on BOTH sides — a mine and a building estate certify and
audit the same way.

## 18. Stakeholder + Community + Social Licence to Operate

**Sub-domains:** community engagement + consultation; grievance
mechanisms; land access + resettlement; Indigenous/customary rights +
FPIC; local-content + shared-value; transparency of payments to
government; human-rights due diligence (links #2 UNGP); reputation +
stakeholder mapping.

**Standards / bodies:**
- **EITI** (Extractive Industries Transparency Initiative) Standard
  (2023 ed.) — payments-to-government transparency across the value
  chain; beneficial ownership; ~57+ implementing countries (mining/oil
  arm — directly relevant to Tanzania).
- **ICMM Mining Principles** + **IRMA** (Initiative for Responsible
  Mining Assurance) — responsible-mining performance (mining arm).
- **IFC Performance Standards** + **Equator Principles** — project-
  finance E&S standards spanning land acquisition, resettlement,
  community, and biodiversity (apply to BOTH large mining and large
  real-estate/infrastructure projects).
- **UNGP / OECD MNE Guidelines** (human rights — links #2).

**Real URLs:**
- https://eiti.org/
- https://www.icmm.com/en-gb/our-principles/tailings/global-industry-standard-on-tailings-management
- IFC PS: https://www.ifc.org/en/insights-reports/2012/ifc-performance-standards — UNVERIFIED
- IRMA: https://responsiblemining.net/ — UNVERIFIED

**Why it serves both:** Both estates depend on social licence — community
consent, land access, and grievance handling. Project-financed
developments on either side answer to **IFC Performance Standards** +
**Equator Principles** (the shared E&S spine). Mining adds EITI + ICMM +
IRMA + GISTM (tailings); real estate adds resettlement + community-impact
norms. Stakeholder + human-rights due diligence is a shared MD duty.
