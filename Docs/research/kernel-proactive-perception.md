## 1. The classical spine (cognitive science + cybernetics) — the load-bearing theory

These are the foundations a PhD committee would require us to ground in. They are not optional colour; each one supplies a *mechanism* we are currently missing.

### 1.1 Endsley's three-level Situation Awareness (SA) — the canonical decomposition of "perception"

Mica Endsley's model is **the** reference frame for machine situational awareness, and it maps *exactly* onto the PERCEIVE→ORIENT half of INV-D. SA = *"the perception of the elements in the environment within a volume of time and space, the comprehension of their meaning, and the projection of their status in the near future."* ([Endsley, Human Factors 1995; misconceptions review 2015](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1555343415572631))

- **Level 1 — Perception:** monitoring, cue detection, recognition → awareness of the raw situational elements and their current states (a vendor's late-delivery count, a grade reading, an arrears bucket, a licence expiry date).
- **Level 2 — Comprehension:** *synthesis* of disjoint Level-1 elements via pattern recognition, interpretation, evaluation — and crucially **determination of relevance to the actor's goals**. This is where raw cues become *"a supplier is about to fail"* rather than *"delivery #7 was 3 days late."*
- **Level 3 — Projection:** forecast future states from current dynamics — *"at this trend the licence renewal will collide with the rains and the FX window; act in the next 10 days."*

The levels are **hierarchical but interactive**: perception feeds comprehension, comprehension enables projection, and projection *shapes what is perceived next* (goal-driven top-down search). ([Wikipedia: Situation awareness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Situation_awareness); [Endsley 2015 PDF](https://maritimesafetyinnovationlab.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Situation-Awareness-Misconceptions-and-Misunderstandings-Endsley-2015.pdf))

**Why it matters for us:** our current detectors are pure **Level-1** (threshold on one metric → headline). We have almost no **Level-2 comprehension layer** (fuse cost-anomaly + cashflow-dip + offtake-slip into *one* situation "margin is being squeezed from three sides") and our **Level-3 projection** is thin (a forecaster MAE drift signal, not a *situation projection*). Endsley is the skeleton the whole lane should be hung on.

### 1.2 Rosen — Anticipatory Systems: the *definition* of being proactive

Robert Rosen, *Anticipatory Systems* (1985): an anticipatory system is one *"whose present behaviour depends upon **future states or future inputs generated by an internal predictive model** of itself and/or its environment, whose predictions are utilised for purposes of present control."* ([Wikipedia: Anticipatory Systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anticipatory_Systems); [A.H. Louie, Mathematical Foundations 2017](https://ahlouie.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/louie-math-found-anticip-sys-2017.pdf))

This is the philosophical license for INV-D's "before being asked": **the MD must carry an internal model of the estate's near-future and act on the *model's* predictions, not on events that have already happened.** A reactive system waits for the arrears to hit 91 days; an *anticipatory* system projects the arrears trajectory and intervenes at day 40. Rosen's "feed-forward control from an internal model" is the formal antonym of "alerting on a breach."

### 1.3 Friston — Free Energy Principle / Active Inference: surprise as the universal currency of attention

The Free Energy Principle (Karl Friston) reframes perception as **surprise minimisation**: a self-maintaining system carries a generative model of its world and continuously minimises *variational free energy* — the divergence between its model's predictions and incoming sensory data (prediction error). It can do this two ways: **update the model (perceive)** or **act on the world to make the data match the prediction (active inference)**. ([Free energy principle — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_energy_principle); [Friston FEP, deep-learning perspective, PMC 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8871280/); [Active inference & FEP, Feb 2026 notes](https://notes.muthu.co/2026/02/active-inference-and-the-free-energy-principle-how-agents-minimize-surprise-instead-of-maximizing-reward/))

**Why it matters:** this gives us the *single scalar* that should drive attention — **prediction error against the MD's internal estate model.** A signal deserves attention to the degree it *surprises* the model relative to a standing concern. This is far better than per-metric fixed thresholds (which fire on the boring and miss the novel).

### 1.4 Itti & Baldi — Bayesian Surprise: the computable definition of salience

Itti & Baldi operationalised Friston's idea for attention: **salience = Bayesian surprise = the KL divergence between the prior belief and the posterior belief after observing data** — `Surprise(D) = KL(P(M|D) ‖ P(M))`. Empirically, Bayesian surprise *attracts human attention* far better than raw signal magnitude. ([Itti & Baldi, "Bayesian surprise attracts human attention", PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2782645/); [Itti–Koch saliency models, PDF](http://ilab.usc.edu/publications/doc/Itti05noa.pdf))

This is the *implementable* version of §1.3: a fully-specified, computable salience score we can put on every estate signal — **how much did this observation change my belief about a standing concern?** It also gives the bottom-up/top-down split we need: bottom-up = surprise in the raw stream; top-down = goal/concern relevance weighting.

### 1.5 Boyd's OODA loop + Ashby's Law of Requisite Variety — the cybernetic frame

Boyd's **OODA** (Observe → Orient → Decide → Act) is the operational twin of INV-D's PERCEIVE→ORIENT→ORGANIZE→EXECUTE, and Boyd explicitly fused **cybernetics, thermodynamics, and evolutionary epistemology**; the decisive move is *Orient* (synthesis with experience/schemas), and the win condition is "getting inside the opponent's decision cycle." ([OODA loop — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OODA_loop); [Cybernetic aspects of OODA](https://harishsnotebook.com/2019/11/10/the-cybernetic-aspects-of-ooda-loop/))

Ashby's **Law of Requisite Variety**: a regulator can only control a system if it has *at least as much variety* (distinguishable internal states) as the disturbances it must absorb. ([same source](https://harishsnotebook.com/2019/11/10/the-cybernetic-aspects-of-ooda-loop/)) **Implication for our sensor layer:** the MD's perceptual repertoire must be *as rich as the estate's failure modes*. Seven detectors cannot regulate a 24-domain mining estate — the sensor layer's "variety" is currently far below the estate's variety. This is the cybernetic argument for the **estate-radar breadth** we propose in §4.

### 1.6 BDI — *standing concerns* as long-term / interest goals; goal-monitoring as the trigger

In the Belief-Desire-Intention agent tradition, classic BDI is reactive-to-events, but the literature extends it with **long-term goals and "interest goals"** precisely to model how humans hold *background concerns* that are not actively being pursued but continuously *monitor* the world for relevant change. Goal monitoring "verifies that expected effects hold and that context conditions that must hold for the whole duration are satisfied," and deliberation includes *opportunity detection*. ([Representing Long-Term and Interest BDI Goals, Springer](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-642-14843-9_13); [Quantitative Operational Monitoring for BDI Agents, AAMAS 2025](https://www.ifaamas.org/Proceedings/aamas2025/pdfs/p2517.pdf))

This is the formal model for *"standing concerns"*: the MD should hold a small set of **maintenance/interest goals** (cash-solvent, licence-valid, counterparties-healthy, grade-stable, margin-protected, workforce-safe, treasury-hedged) and run **goal-monitoring** over the event stream so any signal that threatens a maintenance goal *or* opens an opportunity gets surfaced — even though nobody asked.

### 1.7 Strategic foresight: Ansoff's weak-signal / Strategic Early-Warning System + horizon scanning

Igor Ansoff (1975, *"Managing Strategic Surprise by Response to Weak Signals"*) is the management-science origin of anticipating discontinuities from signals *"not yet strong enough to be heard clearly."* Horizon scanning descends directly from Ansoff's Strategic Early Warning System and from environmental scanning: *systematically scan for weak/emerging/novel signals, then continuously monitor, verify, and assess their growing significance.* ([Springer: strategic foresight & the power of weak signals, 2025](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11625-025-01786-5); [Futures Alchemist: Signal Detection](https://www.futuresalchemist.com/tools-and-models/signal-detection); [NCBI: Horizon scanning & foresight methods](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK556423/))

This is the discipline for the **non-obvious, slow-moving, external** class — a regulatory tightening in Dodoma, an LME grade-premium shift, a buyer's parent company entering distress. Threshold detectors will *never* catch these; weak-signal horizon scanning is a distinct mode that must run alongside the fast anomaly detectors.

## 2. The engineering toolbox (how to actually compute "what to worry about")

### 2.1 Streaming change/anomaly detection — the math for Level-1 perception done right

The right primitives for an always-on numeric sensor are **online changepoint and anomaly detection**, not fixed thresholds:

- **Bayesian Online Changepoint Detection (BOCPD):** maintains a posterior over *run-length* (time since the last regime change) with constant time/space per step — purpose-built for unbounded streams, univariate and multivariate. ([Adams & MacKay BOCPD; survey + recent extensions](https://eecs.wsu.edu/~cook/pubs/kais16.2.pdf); [Bayesian online collective-anomaly + changepoint, 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.06385v1))
- **CUSUM / Page's test (+ Wild Binary Segmentation):** cumulative-sum detection of mean shifts; WBS catches short-lived/localised changes by evaluating CUSUM over random subintervals. ([Probabilistic CUSUM](https://towardsdatascience.com/probabilistic-cusum-for-change-point-detection-121f793ab3a1/); [survey of CP methods, WSU](https://eecs.wsu.edu/~cook/pubs/kais16.2.pdf))
- **Streaming anomaly families** (HTM/Numenta-style, ESD, probabilistic predictive coding) for real-time scoring on a budget. ([Real-time anomaly detection for streaming analytics](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1607.02480); [Online changepoint detection on a budget](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.03710))

These give us *statistically principled* "this trend just changed regime" instead of `ratio > 1.5`. A grade-trend detector built on BOCPD says "the head-grade series entered a new (lower) regime 6 shifts ago with 0.9 posterior" — a real Level-3 weak-signal surfacing.

### 2.2 Complex Event Processing (CEP) — pattern detection across *correlated* streams (Level-2 in infrastructure)

CEP matches continuously incoming events against **patterns/sequences across multiple streams** to detect composite situations that no single metric reveals, in real time — the backbone of fraud detection and **Business Activity Monitoring**, whose explicit goal is *"identifying problems and opportunities in early stages."* ([Complex event processing — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_event_processing); [Databricks: What is CEP](https://www.databricks.com/blog/what-is-complex-event-processing); [Beyond Process Monitoring: event-driven BAM](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/259639864_Beyond_Process_Monitoring_A_Proof-of-Concept_of_Event-driven_Business_Activity_Management))

This is the architectural pattern for our **comprehension layer**: a rule/pattern engine over the estate event stream that fires when *a sequence of facts across domains* forms a known dangerous shape (e.g. `vendor_delivery_late ×3` THEN `vendor_invoice_dispute` THEN `vendor_credit_rating_drop` within 30d → "supplier failing"). CEP is the industrial realisation of Endsley Level-2.

### 2.3 Interruptibility / Value-of-Information — the gate that turns a *concern* into a *surfaced* concern

The hardest part of proactivity is **when to speak**. HCI has 20+ years on this: model the **expected cost of interruption** and the user's **interruptibility intensity**, then *negotiate/mediate/schedule* delivery (immediate / negotiated / mediated / scheduled) rather than firing on availability of information. Classifiers from user-labelled data reach >75% accuracy on interruptibility. ([Roda & Nabeth, Attention economy](https://www.interruptions.net/literature/Roda-AIM06.pdf); [Survey of attention-management systems, arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.06771); [Predicting interruptibility intensity of mobile users](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/316708988_How_Busy_Are_You_Predicting_the_Interruptibility_Intensity_of_Mobile_Users))

For us this becomes a **Value-of-Information × interruptibility gate**: `surface_now = (expected_value_of_acting_early − cost_of_interruption_now) > 0`. Trivial-but-true items go to the daily digest; a cash-cliff in 7 days interrupts immediately. We already have a `fatigue-tracker` — that is the seed of this gate, but it is throttling, not VoI-optimising.

## 3. The 2025–2026 frontier (proactive agents — directly comparable systems)

### 3.1 PROBE (Pasternak et al., Oct 2025, rev Feb 2026) — the canonical *definition + benchmark* of agent proactivity

PROBE ("Proactive Resolution of Bottlenecks") decomposes proactivity into a **three-stage pipeline**: (1) **search for unspecified issues**, (2) **identify the specific bottleneck**, (3) **execute the appropriate resolution**. Best end-to-end score is only **40%**, achieved by *both* GPT-5 and Claude Opus-4.1 — frontier models still **struggle** to be genuinely proactive, especially because real benchmarks demand *"reasoning across sources and longer time horizons,"* not localised context. ([PROBE, arXiv 2510.19771](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.19771))

**This is the most important external result for this lane.** Its three-stage pipeline maps cleanly onto Endsley (search≈perceive across sources, identify≈comprehend the bottleneck, execute≈project+act), and the **40% ceiling proves that "search across sources + long horizons" is the bottleneck** — exactly the cross-domain comprehension + projection layers we lack. It also gives us a ready-made *eval shape* to define "done" for this lane.

### 3.2 Proactive Agent + ProactiveBench (Lu et al., Tsinghua/Renmin/Huawei, 2024) — the *gym* and the *reward model*

Architecture: **Environment Perception** (monitor user activities `A_t`, environment events `E_t`, state `S_t`) → **Intention Inference** `P_t = f_θ(E_t, A_t, S_t)` (predict a task *or null*) → **Proactive Action**. They build an **"Environment Gym"** that *simulates* event streams to train and evaluate, and a **reward model** (fine-tuned Qwen2-7B) that reaches **66.47% F1** on ProactiveBench (beating GPT-4o's 64.60%), with a separate acceptance predictor at **91.8% F1**. Decision logic is framed as **Correct-Detection / False-Detection / Non-Response** — i.e. the model is explicitly trained to *stay silent* when no help is needed. ([Proactive Agent, arXiv 2410.12361](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.12361); [ProactiveBench / project](https://proactllm.github.io/))

**Takeaways for us:** (a) the `predict task or **null**` formulation and the **False-Detection / Non-Response** metrics are the right contract for "don't be annoying"; (b) a **reward/acceptance model** is the learnable interruptibility gate (§2.3); (c) an **event gym** is how we can *eval and improve* our detectors offline against replayed estate streams.

### 3.3 PASK — intent-aware proactive agents grounded in **long-term memory** (Xie et al., Apr 2026)

PASK ("Toward Intent-Aware Proactive Agents with Long-Term Memory", arXiv 2604.08000) ties proactivity to **persisted long-term user intent/memory** so suggestions are grounded in *standing* concerns rather than the last message. ([PASK, arXiv 2604.08000](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.08000)) (PDF body not machine-extractable; cited from title/metadata + the search-result framing in [the proactive-agents survey set](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.08000).)

**This is the bridge to our memory-v2 work.** §1.6's "standing concerns" must live in durable memory (which we *just* made Drizzle-backed, MEM-01/02). PASK is the 2026 confirmation that *the proactive trigger should read from long-term memory of intent*, not from a stateless rule.

### 3.4 Industry direction (2026) — "ambient", always-on, background daemons

The 2026 enterprise pattern is explicitly **ambient / always-on agentic teammates** that "continuously observe their environments," run **background daemons that poll APIs and monitor health**, and surface intelligence with *"shorter routes to usable intelligence"* (less tap-to-ask). ([Salesforce 2026 AI-agent predictions](https://www.salesforce.com/uk/news/stories/the-future-of-ai-agents-top-predictions-trends-to-watch-in-2026/); [Druid AI agentic trends 2026](https://www.druidai.com/blog/ai-trends-in-2026)) Our "all backend, user only chats" mandate is squarely on this trajectory — the differentiator is *depth of estate comprehension*, not the always-on plumbing (which everyone will have).

For each foundation, the leap that takes us past 2026 baseline. The synthesis target is a single organ I'll call the **Estate Radar**: an always-on, surprise-driven, Endsley-layered sensor mesh that converts raw estate signals into a *ranked queue of reasoned concerns*, each one a Level-3 projection tied to a standing concern, gated by VoI.

**Our GAPS vs the SOTA above (the lane's punch-list):**

1. **No comprehension layer (Endsley Level-2) — detectors are siloed Level-1.** Each detector fires on one metric; nothing *fuses* cost-anomaly + cashflow-dip + offtake-slip into one situation "margin pincer." `proactive-alert-engine` hints at it but works off pre-built snapshots, not a live CEP pattern engine. **→ build a cross-domain situation-fusion / CEP pattern layer (L-9).** *(Closest register row: COG-15 / ORCH-situation "SituationalSelfModel"; this lane is its outward-facing twin.)*

3. **No online changepoint detection.** No BOCPD/CUSUM; all detectors are point-in-time threshold checks. A slow grade drift or a regime change in throughput is invisible until it crosses an arbitrary line. **→ streaming changepoint detectors per key series (L-8).**

So today the radar literally cannot see *any* opportunity, and can't see cost/SLO/compliance/vendor anomalies. **→ register the remaining 4 anomaly + 3 opportunity detectors; this is a `detector-registry.ts` one-import-each fix, the cheapest high-leverage win in the lane.**

The MD cannot *add* a concern after a near-miss. **→ a `StandingConcern` table + goal-monitor reading memory-v2 (ties to PASK §3.3 and our fresh MEM-01/02 Drizzle stores).**

6. **No horizon-scanning / weak-signal lane (Ansoff L-7).** The regulator-feed adapters exist but are **unregistered** (register rows KI-16/KI-17: "no standing regulatory-change sensor; real regulator-feed adapter not registered"). So the entire *external slow-signal* class is unobserved. **→ wire the horizon-scan lane + write-back to corpus (KI-16/17 closure).**

7. **No principled interruptibility / VoI gate — only fatigue throttling.** `fatigue-tracker` suppresses by frequency, not by *value of interrupting now*. There is no learned accept/dismiss reward model. **→ a VoI × interruptibility gate, trained on owner feedback (L-10, ProactiveBench-style).**

8. **Cadence is fixed-tier polling, not event-stream-driven.** `tick-cadences` is hot/warm/cold cron; the radar reacts on the next tick, not on the event. Register row **EA-07** confirms: *"Ambient runtime doesn't subscribe estate event streams (no spawn-before-need)."* **→ make the radar a subscriber of `event_outbox` + ledger/licence/FX/KYC streams (EA-07 closure; CEP needs a real stream).**

9. **No projection objects (Endsley Level-3 / Rosen L-2).** Detectors emit a *current-state* headline; almost none emit a *near-future trajectory with lead-time-to-act*. Only `slo-breach` touches forecaster drift. **→ attach a projection (trajectory + intervention deadline) to each concern via the estate world-model (ties RSS-17 / world-model).**

10. **No eval/gym to define "done" or to improve detectors.** No replay of past estate streams, no PROBE-style search→identify→execute scoring, no False-Detection/Non-Response metric. **→ an estate event-gym + PROBE-shaped eval harness (L-11; complements the Wave-D AUT-06 replay→eval→update loop).**

**Net assessment:** we have a *good reactive alerting skeleton* (detectors + scheduler + delivery + fatigue + autonomy gate) but it is **Endsley-Level-1 only, threshold-based, polling-driven, with opportunities entirely dark and the external/weak-signal + comprehension + projection + surprise layers absent.** The lane's work is to upgrade this skeleton into the **Estate Radar**: surprise-driven, stream-subscribed, three-level (perceive→comprehend→project), standing-concern-grounded, VoI-gated. The cheapest first move (register the 7+3 dark detectors) and the highest-leverage structural moves (prediction-error bus + CEP comprehension layer + event-stream subscription + standing-concerns registry) are all clearly scoped and tie directly to existing register rows (EA-07, KI-16/17, COG-15, RSS-17, AUT-06).
