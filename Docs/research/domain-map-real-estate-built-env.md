## How to read this map

The real-estate OS spans **four macro-phases of an asset's life** plus **cross-cutting layers**:

1. **Originate** — acquisition, deal-sourcing, due diligence, valuation.
2. **Create** — planning, design (architecture + all engineering disciplines),
   quantity surveying, procurement, construction delivery, project controls.
3. **Operate** — facilities/property management, leasing/tenancy, maintenance,
   PropTech, collections.
4. **Capitalize / Exit** — real-estate finance, REIT/fund ops, portfolio + asset
   management, brokerage, disposition.

Cross-cutting: legal/regulatory, tax/accounting/insurance, ESG/sustainability,
data/geospatial, professional ethics & registration.

## 2. Due Diligence

**What it covers:** the verification campaign between LOI and close, covering
title, survey, environmental, physical, zoning, financial, and legal.

**Sub-domains (the 9 canonical DD categories):**
1. **Title & Survey** — title search, title insurance, chain of title,
   easements, encroachments, encumbrances, liens, restrictive covenants,
   ALTA/NSPS Land Title Survey (US), boundary/cadastral survey, setbacks,
   flood-zone determination.
2. **Zoning & Entitlements** — zoning designation, permitted uses, variances,
   Conditional Use Permits (CUP), entitlement timeline, planning history,
   development rights, air rights, density/FAR.
3. **Environmental** — Phase I ESA (ASTM E1527-21), Phase II ESA (soil/
   groundwater sampling), Recognized Environmental Conditions (RECs),
   wetlands, endangered species, asbestos/lead/radon surveys, contaminated-land
   remediation, EIA/NEMA approval (East Africa).
4. **Geotechnical & Soils** — soil bearing capacity, slope stability,
   liquefaction, ground investigation (per Eurocode 7 / BS 5930).
5. **Utilities & Infrastructure** — water, sewer, power, gas, telecoms capacity
   & connection rights, way-leaves.
6. **Engineering & Architecture review** — structural/condition assessment,
   Property Condition Assessment (PCA / ASTM E2018).
7. **Construction & Permits** — building permits, occupancy certificates,
   code-compliance, outstanding violations.
8. **Financial & Pro Forma** — rent roll, estoppels, SNDAs, operating
   statements, T-12, lease abstraction.
9. **Tax & Incentives** — property-tax assessment, special assessments,
   abatements, TIF, opportunity zones.

**Standards / bodies:** ASTM E1527 (Phase I ESA), ASTM E2018 (PCA), ALTA/NSPS
(US land title surveys), American Land Title Association, NEMA (Kenya/Tanzania
EIA), RICS Building Surveys.

## 3. Valuation & Appraisal

**What it covers:** opinion of value under recognized bases, for transaction,
lending, financial reporting, tax, insurance, litigation, and investment.

**Three approaches → five methods (RICS / IVS):**
- **Market approach** → (1) **Comparable method** (most widely used; market
  rent & market value of commercial/residential).
- **Income approach** → (2) **Investment method** (capitalization / DCF);
  (3) **Profits method** (trade-related property: hotels, pubs, leisure).
- **Cost approach** → (4) **Depreciated Replacement Cost (DRC)** (specialized
  assets, financial-reporting); (5) **Residual method** (development sites →
  Gross Development Value less costs & profit).

**Bases of value (IVS 102):** Market Value, Market Rent, Equitable Value,
Investment Value/Worth, Synergistic Value, Liquidation Value, Fair Value (IFRS 13).

**Sub-domains:**
- Red Book valuation governance — VPS (Valuation Professional Standards) 1–5,
  VPGAs (Valuation Practice Guidance Applications)
- IVS General Standards: IVS 100 Valuation Framework, IVS 101 Scope of Work,
  IVS 102 Bases of Value, IVS 103 Valuation Approaches, IVS 104 Data & Inputs,
  IVS 105 Valuation Models, IVS 106 Documentation & Reporting
- IVS Asset Standards: IVS 400 Real Property Interests, IVS 410 Development
  Property, IVS 300 Plant/Equipment/Infrastructure (relevant to built env.)
- Automated Valuation Models (AVMs), index-based & mass appraisal (CAMA)
- Discounted Cash Flow (DCF), yield/cap-rate analysis, ARGUS modelling
- Hardcore/top-slice, term-and-reversion, equivalent/initial/reversionary yield
- Valuation for secured lending, financial reporting (IFRS/IAS 40), insurance
  reinstatement cost assessment, compulsory purchase/compensation, rating
- Plant & machinery valuation (IVS 300)

**Standards / bodies:** RICS Valuation – Global Standards (Red Book, effective
31 Jan 2025), IVSC International Valuation Standards (effective 31 Jan 2025),
RICS Registered Valuer scheme, ISK valuers (Kenya), Valuers Registration Board.

## 5. Quantity Surveying & Cost Management

**What it covers:** measurement, cost estimating, cost planning, procurement,
post-contract cost control, whole-life costing — cradle-to-grave.

**Sub-domains (NRM suite, cradle-to-grave):**
- **NRM1** — Order of cost estimating & cost planning for capital building works
  (incl. preliminaries, OH&P, risk allowances, inflation)
- **NRM2** — Detailed measurement for building works → Bills of Quantities (BOQ),
  quantified schedules of works, schedules of rates (replaced SMM7)
- **NRM3** — Order of cost estimating & cost planning for **building maintenance**
- Pre-contract: feasibility estimates, elemental cost plans, cost benchmarking
- Tendering & procurement strategy, tender analysis, contractor selection
- Post-contract: valuations/interim payments, variations, change orders, claims,
  loss & expense, extension-of-time, final account
- Whole-life costing (WLC) / life-cycle costing (ISO 15686), value engineering
- Cost-value reconciliation (CVR), cash-flow forecasting
- Dispute avoidance, adjudication, expert witness (construction)

**Standards / bodies:** RICS NRM1/NRM2/NRM3, RICS Quantity Surveying &
Construction professional group, ISO 15686 (service life planning), Institute
of Quantity Surveyors of Kenya (IQSK), BORAQS (QS registration, Kenya), AAK/QS
chapter, AIQS (Australia comparator).

## 12. Real-Estate Finance, Mortgage & Capital Stack

**What it covers:** how deals are funded across the risk/return spectrum.

**Sub-domains (capital stack, senior → junior):**
- **Senior debt:** commercial bank loans, life-insurance lenders, agency lenders
  (Fannie Mae / Freddie Mac), **CMBS** (commercial mortgage-backed securities);
  LTV typically 60–75%, DSCR 1.20–1.40x
- **Mezzanine debt:** subordinate to senior, ahead of equity; combined LTV cap
  75–85%, rates ~10–18%
- **Preferred equity:** fixed-priority return ahead of common equity
- **Common equity:** sponsor + LP capital, residual upside
- **Underwriting metrics:** LTV, LTC, DSCR, debt yield, cap rate, cash-on-cash,
  IRR, equity multiple, NOI, NPV
- **Development finance:** construction loans, drawdowns, interest reserve,
  bridge-to-perm, land/acquisition finance
- **Residential mortgage:** origination, affordability, LTV, fixed/variable,
  securitization (RMBS)
- **Structured / alternative:** JV equity, syndication, crowdfunding,
  preferred return / waterfall / promote (carried interest), Islamic finance
  (Ijara/Murabaha), green/sustainability-linked loans

**Standards / bodies:** Basel III (bank capital), IFRS 9 (financial
instruments), IVS 500 Financial Instruments, CREFC (CRE Finance Council),
local mortgage regulators (FCA/CFPB; Central Bank of Kenya / BoT prudential
guidelines), valuation-for-secured-lending (RICS).
