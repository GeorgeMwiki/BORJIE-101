## 0. The mental model: a mining estate is six interlocking systems

A world-class mining house is not "a mine." It is six systems run as one P&L:

1. **The geological asset** — ground, tenure, resources, reserves (the thing you own).
2. **The conversion engine** — plan → extract → process → refine → ship (the thing that turns rock into cash).
3. **The commercial book** — marketing, off-take, trading, hedging, treasury, project finance (the thing that prices and protects the cash).
4. **The licence to operate** — fiscal/royalty compliance, HSE, environment, community, ESG, transparency (the thing that lets you keep operating).
5. **The enterprise** — workforce, procurement, maintenance/asset management, holdings, subsidiaries, ancillary businesses (the thing that runs the company).
6. **The dynasty** — family office, succession, the consolidated asset register, governance (the thing that outlives the founder).

The autonomous MD's job is to keep all six green simultaneously, with a coherent KPI spine (Section 14) tying them together. The sections below define "world-class" for each.

## 1. Tenement / Licence Lifecycle & Land Management

**Why it is the foundation:** you cannot finance, mine, or sell what you do not securely hold. Tenure security is the precondition for every downstream activity; lenders explicitly price it.

### 1.1 The licence ladder (CRIRSCO/African pattern)
A mineral right progresses through a ladder of escalating commitment, each tier with distinct work-program, reporting, area-reduction ("relinquishment"), and fee obligations:

- **Reconnaissance / prospecting** → **Exploration / retention** → **Mining (large- vs small-scale)** → **Processing / refining / dealer** licences → **Closure / relinquishment**.
- Kenya's Mining Act 2016 is the modern template: it "regulates prospecting, mining, processing, refining, treatment, transport and any dealings in minerals," splits rights into **large-scale vs small-scale**, and introduced a dedicated **Artisanal Mining Permit** issued at county level — a deliberate formalization on-ramp. ([Kenya Law — Mining Act 2016](https://kenyalaw.org/kl/fileadmin/pdfdownloads/Acts/MiningAct_No12of2016.pdf); [The Lawyer Africa — Kenya licensing](https://thelawyer.africa/2023/10/05/regulation-of-mining-licensing-exploration-and-state-participation/))

### 1.2 The cadastre is the system of record (world-class = digital + transparent)
A **mining cadastre** is the authoritative, spatial register of who holds what ground. Best practice is a rules-engine cadastre that automates the *entire* licence workflow.
- **FlexiCadastre / Landfolio** (Trimble / Spatial Dimension) is used by **~60% of African countries** and "manage[s] rule-based actions including all payments, approvals, public participations, requirements, deadlines, financial reports, work program reports, communications, notifications and other workflow related items that can be associated with a license." ([Spatial Dimension — Mapping Africa](https://www.spatialdimension.com/articles/mapping-africa-mining-cadastre-key-to-unlocking-mineral-wealth))
- World-class jurisdictions run **online self-service cadastres**: apply, pay, renew, submit reports, and view licence maps online (Mozambique, Malawi public portal). Transparency of tenure "enhances investor confidence … ensuring transparency around security of land tenure." ([Spatial Dimension](https://www.spatialdimension.com/articles/mapping-africa-mining-cadastre-key-to-unlocking-mineral-wealth); [Mining in Malawi](https://mininginmalawi.com/mining-in-malawi-302/))

**Capability for the MD:** maintain a live, spatial tenure register; track every licence's *next obligation* (annual fee, work-program report, relinquishment date, renewal window) and never let one lapse — lapse = forfeiture.

### 1.3 Land access ≠ mineral title
Securing mineral rights is separate from securing **surface access**: land acquisition, compensation, resettlement, and overlapping community/pastoralist/agricultural claims. ICMM's 2024 Mining Principles strengthen **resettlement** and **community grievance-mechanism** requirements (Section 4.1). Surface conflict is the #1 cause of mid-tier African project delays.

## 2. Exploration → Resource / Reserve Estimation (the CRIRSCO codes)

This is the discipline that converts "we think there's gold" into a balance-sheet asset a bank will lend against.

### 2.1 The CRIRSCO family — one global grammar
**CRIRSCO** (Committee for Mineral Reserves International Reporting Standards) is the umbrella that harmonizes national codes so "the definitions used in the extractive industries are globally consistent," insisting that **15 standard definitions** apply across all codes. ([CRIRSCO](https://crirsco.com/); [CIM — International Reporting Codes](https://mrmr.cim.org/en/links/international-reporting-codes-for-exploration-results-mineral-resources-and-reserves-definitions/)) The **International Reporting Template (IRT)** "draws on the best of the CRIRSCO-style reporting standards." ([CRIRSCO IRT PDF](https://crirsco.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/isr_mineral_resources_reserves0908.pdf))

The MD must know which code applies by jurisdiction/listing:

| Code | Geography / authority | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **JORC Code 2012** | Australasia (ASX/NZX listing rules) | [jorc.org](https://www.jorc.org/) · [JORC 2012 PDF](https://www.jorc.org/docs/JORC_code_2012.pdf) |
| **NI 43-101 + CIM Definition Standards (2014)** | Canada (TSX) | [NI 43-101 PDF](https://mrmr.cim.org/media/1017/national-instrument-43-101.pdf) · [CIM 2014 PDF](https://mrmr.cim.org/media/1128/cim-definition-standards_2014.pdf) |
| **SAMREC 2016** | South Africa (JSE; SAIMM/GSSA) | [SAMCODE/CRIRSCO](https://www.samcode.co.za/samcodes-standards-committee-ssc/international-alignment/crirsco) |
| **PERC Reporting Standard** | Europe | [Wikipedia — PERC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PERC_Reporting_Standard) |
| **SME Guide** | USA | [CRIRSCO](https://crirsco.com/) |
| Plus CBRR (Brazil), KAZRC (Kazakhstan), KOMPERS (Indonesia), NAEN (Russia), UMREK (Turkey), Chile Cert. Code, Colombia CCRR, Mongolia MPIGM | National | [CRIRSCO about](https://crirsco.com/about-us/) |

### 2.2 The classification ladder (confidence increasing left→right)
```
Exploration Results  →  Mineral Resources              →  Mineral (Ore) Reserves
                         Inferred → Indicated → Measured     Probable ← Proved
                         (geological confidence)             (+ Modifying Factors → economic)
```
- Categorization depends on **(a) confidence in geological information, (b) quality/quantity of data, (c) detail of technical/economic info, (d) interpretation of the data.** ([CIM 2014 standards](https://mrmr.cim.org/media/1128/cim-definition-standards_2014.pdf))
- **Resource ≠ Reserve.** A Resource becomes a Reserve only after applying **Modifying Factors** (mining, processing, metallurgical, infrastructure, economic, marketing, legal, environmental, social, governmental) — typically demonstrated via a feasibility/pre-feasibility study. JORC distinguishes Resources (geological) from Ore Reserves (economically mineable). ([JORC 2012 PDF](https://www.jorc.org/docs/JORC_code_2012.pdf))
- Only **Indicated + Measured** Resources can convert to Reserves; **Inferred** cannot be used in economic studies that underpin Reserves.

### 2.3 The Competent / Qualified Person (the human accountability anchor)
- Public reporting must be signed off by a **Competent Person** (JORC/SAMREC) or **Qualified Person** (NI 43-101) — a named, suitably-experienced, professionally-accountable individual. ([jorc.org](https://www.jorc.org/); [CRIRSCO](https://crirsco.com/))
- Under NI 43-101, "disclosure of mineral resource and mineral reserve estimates may only be made if the estimates use the categories in the CIM Definition Standards." ([CIM](https://mrmr.cim.org/en/standards/canadian-mineral-resource-and-mineral-reserve-definitions/))

### 2.4 The three principles all CRIRSCO codes share
**Transparency · Materiality · Competence.** ([CRIRSCO](https://crirsco.com/)) These are the reporting equivalents of Borjie's evidence-required-AI rule: every estimate must be transparent (reader has enough info to judge it), material (all relevant info included), and competent (signed by an accountable expert). The JORC **Table 1** checklist ("if not, why not") operationalizes this.

**Capability for the MD:** hold resources/reserves as a *graded confidence inventory*, know the code that governs each asset, track Competent-Person sign-off and validity dates, and never let an unsupported number reach an investor surface.

## 3. The Conversion Engine: Plan → Extract → Process → Refine

### 3.1 Mine planning & scheduling (the NPV optimizer)
World-class planning is a multi-horizon optimization stack, all driving **NPV** subject to physical and tenure constraints:
- **Strategic / Life-of-Mine (LOM):** pit/stope optimization (Lerchs-Grossmann shells), cutoff-grade strategy, production-rate selection. Tools: **GEOVIA Whittle**, **Hexagon MinePlan**, **Maptek**, **Datamine**. ([Dassault GEOVIA Whittle](https://www.3ds.com/products/geovia/whittle); [Hexagon MinePlan](https://hexagon.com/products/hexagon-mineplan-op-engineering-long-term))
- **Cutoff-grade optimization is a value lever, not an accounting choice:** "Cutoff grade strategies that initially use high grades and decline over time have shown to significantly improve NPVs." Dynamic schedulers find "N-best yearly cut-off grade and production rate policies that maximize NPV over the life of the mine while respecting precedence constraints." ([ThreeDify](https://threedify.com/cut-off-grade-optimizer/); [Maptek heuristics PDF](https://www.maptek.com/pdf/insight/AUSIMM_Applying_Modern_Heuristics.pdf))
- **Underground** uses mixed-integer programming with variable cutoff grades. ([Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11081-019-09479-6))
- The prize is real: Chuquicamata improved NPV by **US$800M** through optimization. ([Academia.edu — open pit optimization](https://www.academia.edu/28773315/Open_Pit_Optimization_Strategies_for_Improving_Economics_of_Mining_Projects_Through_Mine_Planning))
- **Short-term planning** reconciles the strategic plan to weekly/monthly reality (blending, stockpile management, equipment dispatch). ([PMC — short-term optimization](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12498321/))

### 3.2 Extraction methods (the MD must match method to ore body)
- **Surface:** open-pit, strip, alluvial/placer (dominant for Tanzanian/East-African ASM gold), dredging, in-situ leach.
- **Underground:** room-and-pillar, cut-and-fill, sublevel stoping/caving, block caving (for large low-grade), longwall (coal).
- Method selection is governed by depth, grade distribution, geomechanics, strip ratio, and capital intensity — and feeds back into the cutoff/schedule model.

### 3.3 Ore processing / beneficiation / metallurgy
"Mineral processing is the art and technology of treating ores to separate valuable minerals from waste rock, with the three main processes being leaching, flotation and gravity concentration." ([911 Metallurgist](https://www.911metallurgist.com/blog/flotation-2/))

The canonical flowsheet:
1. **Comminution** (crush → grind/mill): liberate value minerals. "Fine enough to liberate … but coarse enough to avoid overgrinding" — the single biggest energy cost, optimized per-orebody by test work + mineralogy. ([AHK — Rock to Revenue](https://www.ahkgroup.com/from-rock-to-revenue-the-power-of-mineral-processing/))
2. **Concentration / separation** — chosen by ore type: **gravity**, **froth flotation**, **leaching (CIL/CIP/heap)**, **magnetic**, **electrostatic**. ([Multotec](https://www.multotec.com/en/mineral-ore-processing))
3. **Recovery economics** — recovery is the metallurgical yield KPI: e.g., ">90% recovery using gravity, up to 99% recovery by flotation of the gravity tail"; gold sulphide circuits combine **gravity + flotation + Carbon-in-Leach (CIL)**. ([911 Metallurgist — gravity/flotation/CIL](https://www.911metallurgist.com/equipment/gravity-gold-recovery-rougher-concentrate-flotation-cil-leaching/))

**Mercury caveat for ASM (Tanzania-critical):** artisanal gold recovery via mercury amalgamation is the sector's worst environmental/health externality; formalization programs train miners to operate **mercury-free** (gravity + borax, retorts) per the **Minamata Convention**. ([IGF — Formalizing ASM](https://www.igfmining.org/formalizing-artisanal-and-small-scale-mining-for-inclusive-sustainable-development/))

### 3.4 Value-addition / refining / smelting (and the Tanzanian mandate)
- **Smelting** (pyrometallurgy: concentrate → blister/matte) and **refining** (electrolytic → cathode/doré → bullion) capture downstream margin. For copper, this is where **TC/RC** economics live (Section 5.3).
- **Tanzania legally forces in-country value addition:** the government took "a lien in all mineral concentrates," the Mining Commission must "analyze and value concentrates for processing within Tanzania," and "transportation of concentrates outside Tanzania for processing is no longer allowed." ([Mondaq — Tanzania amendments](https://www.mondaq.com/mining/677170/amendments-to-the-mining-act-in-tanzania)) The MD's flowsheet decisions are therefore *constrained by law*, not just economics, in TZ.

## 4. The Licence to Operate: HSE, ESG, Environment, Transparency

This is where mid-tier operators most often fail and where world-class operators differentiate. The autonomous MD must treat these as hard constraints, not soft goals.

### 4.1 ICMM Mining Principles (the global ESG operating standard)
The **10 ICMM Mining Principles** (originally 2003, **updated December 2024**) define good-practice ESG for the industry; ICMM members ("29 major companies + 36 associations") must implement them with **site-level validation, credible assurance, and transparent disclosure** (validation required since 2020). ([ICMM Mining Principles](https://www.icmm.com/en-gb/our-principles/mining-principles/mining-principles); [Mining Journal](https://www.mining-journal.com/leadership/news/1381049/icmm-updates-principles-responsible-mining))

The ten (titles): **(1) Ethical business · (2) Decision-making (integrate sustainability) · (3) Human rights · (4) Risk management · (5) Health & safety · (6) Environmental performance · (7) Conservation of biodiversity · (8) Responsible production · (9) Social performance · (10) Stakeholder engagement.** ([ICMM via search](https://www.icmm.com/en-gb/our-principles/mining-principles/mining-principles))

The 2024 update strengthened: **labour rights, resettlement, gender, access to grievance mechanisms, mine closure, pollution and waste.** ([Mining Journal](https://www.mining-journal.com/leadership/news/1381049/icmm-updates-principles-responsible-mining))

### 4.2 Health & Safety: the KPI spine and the prevention method
- **Lagging indicators:** **TRIFR** (Total Recordable Injury Frequency Rate), **LTIFR** (Lost Time), fatalities — all expressed **per million hours worked**. ICMM's best recorded **TRIFR = 2.29 in 2024** (down from 5.07 in 2012). ([ICMM via search](https://www.icmm.com/en-gb/research/health-safety/benchmarking-2024-safety-data))
- **Critical Control Management (CCM) + bowtie** is the SOTA *leading* approach to prevent fatalities: identify the controls that matter most, set performance expectations, verify them in the field, assign ownership, report status. **In 2024, failure to implement effective critical controls accounted for 83% of fatalities.** ([ICMM CCM](https://www.icmm.com/en-gb/our-work/health-and-safety/critical-control-management); [Discovery Alert — CCM 2026](https://discoveryalert.com.au/icmm-critical-control-management-guide-mining-safety-2026/))

### 4.3 Tailings: the catastrophic-risk standard
- The **Global Industry Standard on Tailings Management (GISTM, Aug 2020)** — co-convened by **UNEP, PRI, ICMM** after Brumadinho (25 Jan 2019) — has **6 topic areas, 15 principles, 77 auditable requirements**, mapped by **219 conformance criteria**, targeting **zero harm**. ICMM members committed: "extreme/very high" consequence facilities conformant by **Aug 2023**, all others by **Aug 2025**. ([Global Tailings Review PDF](https://globaltailingsreview.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/global-industry-standard-on-tailings-management.pdf); [UNEP](https://www.unep.org/news-and-stories/press-release/new-global-industry-standard-tailings-management-aims-improve-safety); [ICMM conformance protocols](https://www.icmm.com/en-gb/our-principles/tailings/tailings-conformance-protocols))

### 4.4 Climate / decarbonisation
- ICMM members committed to **net-zero Scope 1 & 2 by 2050 or sooner**, with **short/medium-term targets**, **accelerated Scope 3 action**, **annual reporting with external verification**, aligned to **TCFD (by 2023)**. ([ICMM net-zero](https://www.icmm.com/en-gb/our-work/decarbonisation/net-zero-commitment); [IM-Mining](https://im-mining.com/2021/10/05/icmm-members-pledge-to-reach-net-zero-by-2050-or-sooner/))

### 4.5 Transparency & anti-corruption: EITI 2023
- The **2023 EITI Standard** mandates **contract & licence disclosure** (everything granted/amended from **1 Jan 2021**), and strengthened **beneficial-ownership** rules: **PEP disclosure regardless of ownership level**, recommended **≤10% reporting threshold**, **SOE** ownership detail, and **full ownership chains** for indirect holdings. ([EITI Standard 2023 PDF](https://eiti.org/sites/default/files/2023-06/2023%20EITI%20Standard.pdf); [Open Ownership](https://www.openownership.org/en/news/2023-eiti-standard-strengthened-requirements-on-beneficial-ownership-transparency/))

### 4.6 Responsible sourcing / supply-chain due diligence
- **OECD Due Diligence Guidance for Responsible Supply Chains of Minerals from Conflict-Affected and High-Risk Areas** is the global baseline; its supplement calls on stakeholders "to engage in the legalization and formalization of artisanal and small-scale mining," and is embedded in DRC/Burundi/Rwanda law. ([OECD DDG PDF](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2021/02/oecd-due-diligence-guidance-for-responsible-supply-chains-of-minerals-from-conflict-affected-and-high-risk-areas_13d8673f/e9a65a5d-en.pdf); [OECD responsible minerals](https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/sub-issues/due-diligence-guidance-for-responsible-business-conduct/responsible-mineral-supply-chains.html))

## 5. The Commercial Book: Marketing, Off-take, Trading, Hedging

This is the function that prices the metal and protects the cash — the most under-built capability in artisanal-to-mid-tier operators, and the highest-leverage one for an AI MD.

### 5.1 Off-take agreements (the revenue contract)
An off-take agreement is the long-term sale contract. World-class terms specify: **concentrate quality specs, precious-metals payabilities, treatment & refining charges (TC/RC), and penalties for deleterious elements.** ([Discovery Alert — copper offtake](https://discoveryalert.com.au/copper-concentrate-offtake-agreements-strategies-2026/)) Real-world example: Troilus–Boliden copper-gold concentrate off-take. ([Mining Technology](https://www.mining-technology.com/news/troilus-gold-offtake-terms-boliden-copper-gold-concentrate/))

### 5.2 Streaming & royalty financing (off-take's financing cousins)
"Streaming, royalty and offtake agreements may look similar … but they distribute risk, cashflows and control in different ways." Streams sell future production at a fixed discount for upfront capital — a major emerging finance powerhouse for mid-tier builders. ([Allens — mining streams](https://www.allens.com.au/insights-news/insights/2026/04/mining-streams-a-simple-guide-to-an-emerging-finance-powerhouse/); [Lexology — streaming](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=74a1c442-2a71-4d84-8f0b-2a01c1b1bad6))

### 5.3 Pricing mechanics (the MD must compute net revenue, not gross)
- Copper concentrate pricing links to **LME**, typically capturing **85%–96.5% of LME** after deducting TC/RC. ([Discovery Alert](https://discoveryalert.com.au/copper-concentrate-offtake-agreements-strategies-2026/))
- **TC/RC** = the smelter's fee: "Treatment charges (TCs) are fees paid by mining companies to smelters to convert copper concentrate into blister copper. Refining charges (RCs) cover the subsequent electrolytic refining." They are **benchmarked annually** against global smelter capacity utilisation. ([Discovery Alert — TC/RC](https://discoveryalert.com.au/advanced-concentrate-marketing-2026-mining-frameworks/); [Discovery Alert — Japan TC/RC crisis](https://discoveryalert.com.au/japan-copper-concentrate-integration-tcrc-crisis-ppc-2026/))
- Contracts use **penalty + bonus mechanisms** for grade and deleterious elements (As, Hg, etc.) to manage variability. ([Discovery Alert](https://discoveryalert.com.au/advanced-concentrate-marketing-2026-mining-frameworks/))

### 5.4 Hedging & trading
- Reference pricing is **LME** (base metals) and **LBMA** gold/silver. The MD's hedging book (forwards, collars, options) protects committed capex/debt service against price falls while preserving upside — a treasury-risk discipline, governed by a board-approved hedging policy. (Mechanics **UNVERIFIED** to a single authoritative URL in this pass; LME/LBMA reference confirmed via [Discovery Alert](https://discoveryalert.com.au/copper-concentrate-offtake-agreements-strategies-2026/).)

## 6. Royalty & Fiscal Regime Management

The MD must continuously compute, accrue, and pay the state's share — and model how regime choice changes project economics.

### 6.1 Royalty archetypes (know which one applies)
- **Ad valorem** — % of production value (dominant in Africa/LatAm/Asia-Pacific).
- **Specific / unit** — charge per physical unit.
- **Profit-based** — % of accounting profit / net cash flow.
- **Windfall / graduated price-based** — sliding rate triggered by higher commodity prices.
- **Hybrid** — blends the above. ([IMF eLibrary — international mineral taxation](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/display/book/9780415781381/ch005.xml); [IGF — variable royalties PDF](https://www.igfmining.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/variable-royalties-an-answer-to-volatile-mineral-prices.pdf))
- IGF guidance: a variable royalty "should be considered as one part of the entire fiscal regime." ([IGF variable royalties](https://www.igfmining.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/variable-royalties-an-answer-to-volatile-mineral-prices.pdf)) For full fiscal-term taxonomy see [NRGI — Oil, Gas and Mining Fiscal Terms](https://resourcegovernance.org/analysis-tools/publications/oil-gas-and-mining-fiscal-terms).

### 6.2 Tanzania fiscal regime (Borjie launch jurisdiction — memorize)
The 2017 overhaul is aggressively state-favouring; the MD must model it precisely:
- **Royalty 6%** on metallic minerals (gold, copper, silver, platinum), raised from 4%. ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/mining/677170/amendments-to-the-mining-act-in-tanzania); [Resource Governance briefing](https://resourcegovernance.org/sites/default/files/documents/tanzania-new-natural-legislation-what-will-change.pdf))
- **1% clearing fee** on the value of all minerals exported (from 1 Jul 2017), plus an **inspection fee** payable before export. ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/mining/677170/amendments-to-the-mining-act-in-tanzania))
- **≥16% free carried interest** to government in mining licences; government may acquire **up to 50%** of shares proportional to tax expenditures benefiting the company. ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/mining/677170/amendments-to-the-mining-act-in-tanzania))
- **Local content:** foreign service providers must JV with Tanzanian partners holding **≥51%** (or **≥25%** where goods unavailable). ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/mining/677170/amendments-to-the-mining-act-in-tanzania))
- **In-country processing mandated**; concentrate export banned (Section 3.4).
- **Mining Commission** is the regulator (absorbed the Mining Advisory Board, ex-Commissioner, Zonal Offices, TMAA): issues licences, regulates, resolves disputes, inspects. ([Resource Governance briefing](https://resourcegovernance.org/sites/default/files/documents/tanzania-new-natural-legislation-what-will-change.pdf); official: [Tume ya Madini](https://www.tumemadini.go.tz/publications/regulations/))
- **Permanent sovereignty:** disputes must be settled **within Tanzania**; the state may **renegotiate "unconscionable" terms**. ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/mining/677170/amendments-to-the-mining-act-in-tanzania))

### 6.3 Pan-African expansion regimes (KE / UG / NG)
- **Kenya (Mining Act 2016):** large- vs small-scale rights, Artisanal Mining Permit, draft **Royalty Collection & Management** and **Mineral Royalty Sharing** Regulations 2023 splitting royalties across **National / County / Community** — a three-way share the MD must allocate. ([Kenya Law](https://kenyalaw.org/kl/fileadmin/pdfdownloads/Acts/MiningAct_No12of2016.pdf); [The Lawyer Africa](https://thelawyer.africa/2023/10/05/regulation-of-mining-licensing-exploration-and-state-participation/))
- **Uganda & Nigeria:** frameworks exist (Uganda Mining & Minerals Act 2022; Nigerian Minerals and Mining Act 2007). Specific royalty/licence terms **UNVERIFIED** in this research pass — flag for a dedicated jurisdiction deep-dive before KE/UG/NG go-live; cross-jurisdiction comparison resource: [African Mining Legislation Atlas](https://www.a-mla.org/en/country/Kenya).

## 7. Treasury & Project Finance

### 7.1 Capital structure
High leverage is standard because "the cost of debt financing is much cheaper than the cost of equity," letting equity realize the asset with less capital. ([Discovery Alert — project financing](https://discoveryalert.com.au/project-financing-mining-ventures-2025-approaches/))

### 7.2 Lender coverage ratios (the MD's financing covenants)
- **DSCR ≥ ~1.5x** (cash flow vs debt service), **LLCR 1.7–2.0x**, **PLCR > 2.0x** — thresholds vary by risk. ([Forvis Mazars — DSCR](https://financialmodelling.forvismazars.com/resources/debt-service-coverage-ratio-dscr/))
- **Reserve tail ratio ≥ 30%** — lenders require ≥30% of reserves remain at final repayment (so the mine outlives the loan). ([Discovery Alert — project financing](https://discoveryalert.com.au/project-financing-mining-ventures-2025-approaches/))
- **DSRA** (Debt Service Reserve Account) covers temporary shortfalls when DSCR dips below 1.0x. ([Forvis Mazars](https://financialmodelling.forvismazars.com/resources/debt-service-coverage-ratio-dscr/))

### 7.3 E&S finance gates
Lenders "increasingly demand adherence to … the **Equator Principles** and **IFC Performance Standards**" — a risk-management framework for E&S risk in project finance and "a minimum standard for due diligence." ([Discovery Alert](https://discoveryalert.com.au/project-financing-mining-ventures-2025-approaches/); [Equator Principles — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equator_Principles); [World Bank PPP — EPs](https://ppp.worldbank.org/library/equator-principles-eps)) Borjie's evidence/audit posture maps directly onto these.

## 8. Procurement & Supply Chain

World-class procurement is a controlled, auditable spend engine: **category management, vendor pre-qualification, competitive tender, contract lifecycle, and goods-receipt/3-way match**, with **local-content** quotas baked in (Tanzania's 51%/25% JV rules force this — Section 6.2). Supply-chain due diligence (OECD, Section 4.6) and ICMM Principle 8 (responsible production / supply chain) govern the upstream. Critical-spares strategy is the link to maintenance (Section 10). ## 9. Workforce / HR & Training

- **Role-gated workforce** (owner / manager / employee) is exactly Borjie's `workforce-mobile` model.
- ICMM Principle 3 (human rights) and the 2024 strengthening of **labour rights, gender, and grievance-mechanism access** set the bar. ([Mining Journal](https://www.mining-journal.com/leadership/news/1381049/icmm-updates-principles-responsible-mining))
- For ASM, **formalization = training**: licensed miners "trained on effective environmental and mining practices, and committed to not using mercury." ([IGF — Formalizing ASM](https://www.igfmining.org/formalizing-artisanal-and-small-scale-mining-for-inclusive-sustainable-development/))
- HSE competency, critical-control verification ownership (Section 4.2), and fatigue/contractor management are the workforce-safety links.

## 10. Maintenance / Asset Management (ISO 55000)

- **ISO 55000/55001** is the global asset-management standard: asset management = "the coordinated activity of an organization to realize value from assets," covering lifecycle, risk, and performance, with "structured, evidence-based decision-making across the asset lifecycle." Most adopted in industries where "asset performance has a direct, material impact on safety, revenue, and regulatory compliance: utilities, oil and gas, **mining**, transportation." ([Tractian — ISO 55000](https://tractian.com/en/glossary/iso-55000); [Asset Leadership Network](https://www.assetleadership.net/iso55000-base/iso-55000-asset-management-overview/))
- Reliability methods: **RCM** (Reliability-Centered Maintenance) + **RCA** (Root-Cause Analysis) "evaluate modes of failure and determine their causes"; **condition monitoring** (ISO 13374) feeds predictive maintenance and informs "inspection intervals, life extensions, and capital investment." ([Sensemore](https://sensemore.io/maintenance-standards-of-asset-management-and-reliability/); [Tractian](https://tractian.com/en/glossary/iso-55000))
- For the MD this links **availability/utilization** (Section 14) to capex planning and AISC sustaining-capital.

## 11. Holdings / Subsidiaries & Ancillary Businesses

A mining *estate* (vs a single mine) is a holding structure: mining SPVs per asset, processing/refining entities, logistics, equipment-leasing, power, water, housing, retail/agribusiness ancillaries serving mine communities, and treasury/finance entities. World-class practice: **consolidated reporting up the holding tree**, ring-fenced project SPVs (lender requirement), intercompany pricing discipline, and a single consolidated view of cash and exposure. This is the corporate-structure layer that EITI's **beneficial-ownership chain** disclosure (Section 4.5) now makes legally visible — the MD must keep the ownership graph clean and reportable. ([EITI Standard 2023 PDF](https://eiti.org/sites/default/files/2023-06/2023%20EITI%20Standard.pdf))

## 12. Family Office & Succession

The dynasty layer — the difference between a company and an estate.
- **Governance core:** family **mission statement, family constitution/charter, family council, succession plan** — "an operating framework for a family's financial life," with multi-jurisdiction families advised to set governance "at the family level … rather than … jurisdiction-specific legal entities." ([Creative Planning](https://creativeplanning.com/family-office/family-governance/); [Family Office Advisory](https://family-office-advisory.com/governance-succession))
- **The hard truth:** "up to **95% of wealth transfer failures** are attributed to communication breakdowns, unprepared heirs, and a lack of shared vision, rather than poor financial decisions." ([BPM — generational wealth transfer](https://www.bpm.com/insights/generational-wealth-transfer/))
- **Succession is an 18–36 month structured program** from values articulation → governance handover; **next-gen development is the most under-resourced yet highest-predictor phase.** ([Family Office Advisory — succession process](https://family-office-advisory.com/articles/succession-planning-process-step-by-step))
- **Wealth-transfer instruments:** irrevocable trusts, GRATs, dynasty trusts, family limited partnerships. ([BPM](https://www.bpm.com/insights/generational-wealth-transfer/))

## 13. The Consolidated Asset Register

The single source of truth tying the estate together: every tenement (Section 1), resource/reserve block (Section 2), fixed asset / plant / fleet (ISO 55000, Section 10), subsidiary/holding (Section 11), financial instrument (hedges, streams, debt — Sections 5/7), and family/estate asset (Section 12) — each with location, valuation, ownership, encumbrance, lifecycle status, and the *next obligation* attached. World-class = it reconciles to the consolidated financials and to the cadastre, and it is the substrate the MD's KPIs (Section 14) are computed from. (Composite capability; no single external standard — anchored by ISO 55000 asset-register discipline and EITI ownership-chain disclosure.)

## 14. KPIs / Dashboards — the MD's instrument panel

The autonomous MD must compute and watch these continuously, each cited:

| KPI | What it measures | World-class anchor / source |
|---|---|---|
| **AISC** (All-in Sustaining Cost) | Cash cost + sustaining capex + corporate G&A + reclamation + sustaining exploration, per oz/unit | [WGC AISC](https://www.gold.org/about-gold/gold-supply/responsible-gold/all-in-costs) · [Selborne](https://selborneresearch.com/guides/mining/what-is-aisc/) |
| **AIC** (All-in Cost) | AISC + non-sustaining (growth) capex & exploration | [WGC AISC/AIC](https://www.gold.org/about-gold/gold-supply/responsible-gold/all-in-costs) |
| **Cash cost (C1)** | Direct mining + processing + site G&A, net of by-product credits | [WGC](https://www.gold.org/about-gold/gold-supply/responsible-gold/all-in-costs) |
| **Head grade** | g/t or % metal fed to plant | [Multotec](https://www.multotec.com/en/mineral-ore-processing) |
| **Metallurgical recovery** | % value metal recovered (e.g. 90% gravity, up to 99% flotation) | [911 Metallurgist](https://www.911metallurgist.com/equipment/gravity-gold-recovery-rougher-concentrate-flotation-cil-leaching/) |
| **Throughput** | t/hr or Mt/yr milled vs nameplate | [Hexagon MinePlan](https://hexagon.com/products/hexagon-mineplan-op-engineering-long-term) |
| **Strip ratio** | waste : ore (open pit) | [Academia.edu — open pit optimization](https://www.academia.edu/28773315/Open_Pit_Optimization_Strategies_for_Improving_Economics_of_Mining_Projects_Through_Mine_Planning) |
| **NPV / IRR** | LOM value; the optimization objective | [Dassault Whittle](https://www.3ds.com/products/geovia/whittle) |
| **Reserve/Resource inventory & R:P ratio** | Graded confidence + reserve life | [JORC 2012](https://www.jorc.org/docs/JORC_code_2012.pdf) · [CIM 2014](https://mrmr.cim.org/media/1128/cim-definition-standards_2014.pdf) |
| **TRIFR / LTIFR / fatalities** | Safety, per million hours worked (ICMM TRIFR 2.29 in 2024) | [ICMM safety benchmarking](https://www.icmm.com/en-gb/research/health-safety/benchmarking-2024-safety-data) |
| **Critical-control verification %** | Leading safety (83% of 2024 fatalities = control failure) | [ICMM CCM](https://www.icmm.com/en-gb/our-work/health-and-safety/critical-control-management) |
| **Scope 1/2/3 emissions** | Net-zero-by-2050 trajectory, TCFD-aligned | [ICMM net-zero](https://www.icmm.com/en-gb/our-work/decarbonisation/net-zero-commitment) |
| **DSCR / LLCR / PLCR / reserve-tail** | Financing health (DSCR ≥1.5x, tail ≥30%) | [Forvis Mazars](https://financialmodelling.forvismazars.com/resources/debt-service-coverage-ratio-dscr/) · [Discovery Alert](https://discoveryalert.com.au/project-financing-mining-ventures-2025-approaches/) |
| **Asset availability / utilization / MTBF** | ISO 55000 reliability | [Tractian — ISO 55000](https://tractian.com/en/glossary/iso-55000) |
| **Royalty & fiscal accrual** | State share owed/paid (TZ 6% + 1% clearing) | [Mondaq — Tanzania](https://www.mondaq.com/mining/677170/amendments-to-the-mining-act-in-tanzania) |

**AISC is the master profitability KPI.** WGC defines it as "the sum of cash operating costs, sustaining capital expenditure, and corporate general and administrative expenses … per ounce," explicitly *including* reclamation, sustaining exploration/study, and sustaining development; *excluding* non-sustaining (growth) capex, M&A, impairment, one-time severance, and financing/interest. ([Selborne — AISC](https://selborneresearch.com/guides/mining/what-is-aisc/); [WGC](https://www.gold.org/about-gold/gold-supply/responsible-gold/all-in-costs))
