## PART A — CORE MINING VALUE CHAIN

### A1. Geoscience & Mineral Exploration

- **Sub-domains:** regional/greenfield vs brownfield exploration;
  remote sensing & satellite spectral mapping; airborne & ground
  geophysics (magnetics, gravimetrics, electromagnetics, IP/resistivity,
  seismic); geochemistry (soil/stream-sediment/rock-chip sampling,
  pathfinder elements); structural & economic geology; geological
  modelling; target generation; drill program design; core logging &
  geotechnical logging; QA/QC of assays; data management; 3D seismic +
  AI-assisted deep discovery.
- **Standards / bodies:** SEG (Society of Economic Geologists), AusIMM,
  SGA (Society for Geology Applied to Mineral Deposits), USGS, national
  geological surveys (Geological Survey of Tanzania — GST).
- **Source:** SRK Consulting — Geology https://www.srk.com/en/geology
  **[search-cited]**; USGS geophysical methods
  https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/1995/ofr-95-0831/CHAP3.pdf **[search-cited]**
- **Why associated:** The estate's resource base originates here; every
  downstream valuation, mine plan, and financing depends on exploration
  confidence levels.

### A2. Resource & Reserve Estimation / Public Reporting

- **Sub-domains:** geostatistics (kriging, conditional simulation,
  variography); block modelling; classification (Inferred / Indicated /
  Measured Resources; Probable / Proved Reserves); cut-off grade theory;
  modifying factors; Competent/Qualified Person sign-off; public
  technical-report drafting; resource-to-reserve conversion.
- **Standards / bodies:** **CRIRSCO** (umbrella); **JORC Code**
  (Australasia/ASX); **NI 43-101 + CIM Definition Standards/Guidelines**
  (Canada); **SAMREC** (South Africa); **PERC** (Europe); **SME Guide**
  (USA); **Certification Code** (Chile). JORC 2026 update folds ESG
  (climate, water, land, community) into reporting.
- **Source:** CRIRSCO https://crirsco.com/ **[fetched — member roster
  confirmed]**; SME state-of-codes
  https://me.smenet.org/state-of-codes-and-standards-for-the-reporting-of-mineral-resources-and-reserves/
  **[search-cited]**
- **Why associated:** This is the legally reportable backbone of asset
  value; the OS must store, version, and audit resource statements.

### A3. Mine Planning & Mining Engineering

- **Sub-domains:** strategic vs tactical vs operational planning; pit
  optimisation (Lerchs-Grossmann / pseudoflow); open-pit design (ramps,
  benches, slope angles); underground method selection (room-and-pillar,
  sublevel stoping, block/sublevel caving, cut-and-fill, longwall);
  scheduling; dilution & recovery; geotechnical/rock-mechanics &
  ground-control; ventilation; dewatering; reconciliation;
  grade-control; short-interval control.
- **Standards / bodies:** SME Mining Engineering Handbook; AusIMM;
  ground-control codes per jurisdiction; geotech guidelines.
- **Source:** SRK geostatistics & mine planning
  https://www.srk.com/en/services/geostatistics **[search-cited]**
- **Why associated:** Converts the reserve into a producible, scheduled,
  costed extraction sequence the estate executes against.

### A4. Drill & Blast / Extraction Operations

- **Sub-domains:** drilling pattern design; explosives selection &
  management; blast design & vibration/airblast control; fragmentation
  optimisation; load & haul fleet operations; dispatch & fleet
  management; ROM stockpile management; in-pit/underground logistics;
  dewatering; dust control.
- **Standards / bodies:** ISEE (International Society of Explosives
  Engineers); national explosives & magazine regulations; IEEE/ISO
  vibration limits.
- **Source:** Mining engineering operations references; AusIMM
  **UNVERIFIED**
- **Why associated:** The physical production heartbeat; feeds tonnes &
  grade to processing and drives the cost & safety profile.

### A5. Mineral Processing / Metallurgy / Beneficiation

- **Sub-domains:** comminution (crushing, SAG/ball milling, HPGR);
  classification (cyclones, screens); gravity concentration; magnetic &
  electrostatic separation; froth flotation; dense-media separation;
  **hydrometallurgy** (leaching — heap/CIL/CIP, cyanidation, acid/
  pressure leach, SX-EW); **pyrometallurgy** (roasting, smelting,
  converting, refining); **electrometallurgy** (electrowinning,
  electrorefining); dewatering/thickening/filtration; tailings
  generation; metallurgical accounting & mass balance; ore-sorting;
  process control & instrumentation.
- **Standards / bodies:** SME/AusIMM metallurgical handbooks;
  process-specific best practice; ISO process-control standards.
- **Source:** Mineral processing overview (Wikipedia)
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mineral_processing **[search-cited]**;
  Extractive metallurgy
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extractive_metallurgy **[search-cited]**
- **Why associated:** Determines metal recovery & saleable product
  quality — the value-capture step between ore and revenue.

### A6. Value-Addition / Refining / Smelting / Local Beneficiation

- **Sub-domains:** doré bar production; toll-refining vs integrated
  refining; LBMA Good Delivery refining; gemstone cutting/polishing;
  base-metal cathode production; downstream beneficiation & local-content
  value-addition mandates; by-product & critical-mineral recovery.
- **Standards / bodies:** **LBMA Good Delivery / Responsible Gold
  Guidance**; **LME** brand listings; national value-addition/local-
  content laws (Tanzania local content; beneficiation mandates).
- **Source:** LBMA https://www.lbma.org.uk/ **[search-cited]**
- **Why associated:** Pan-African policy (AMV) and Tanzanian law push
  in-country value-addition; the estate must model the make-vs-export
  decision and its fiscal consequences.

### A7. Assaying, Sampling & Grade Control

- **Sub-domains:** sampling theory (Gy's theory, sampling error);
  fire-assay & instrumental assay (AAS, ICP-OES/MS, XRF); umpire/check
  assays; certified reference materials, blanks & duplicates; laboratory
  accreditation; grade-control modelling; reconciliation (mine-to-mill).
- **Standards / bodies:** **ISO/IEC 17025** (lab competence); national
  metrology bodies; CRMs from certified providers.
- **Source:** ISO/IEC 17025 (ISO) **UNVERIFIED**
- **Why associated:** Assay integrity underpins resource confidence,
  metal accounting, royalty assessment, and dispute resolution.

## PART B — COMMERCIAL, MARKETS & FISCAL

### B1. Mineral Economics & Commodity Markets

- **Sub-domains:** supply/demand fundamentals; commodity price cycles;
  cost curves (C1/C2/AISC — all-in sustaining cost); incentive pricing;
  benchmark & reference pricing; critical-minerals & energy-transition
  demand; substitution & elasticity.
- **Standards / bodies:** **LME** (base metals reference prices);
  **LBMA** (gold/silver benchmark via ICE Benchmark Administration;
  platinum/palladium via LME); World Bank/IMF commodity outlooks.
- **Source:** LME official prices
  https://www.lme.com/market-data/lme-reference-prices/lme-official-price
  **[search-cited]**; LBMA prices
  https://www.lbma.org.uk/prices-and-data/precious-metal-prices
  **[search-cited]**
- **Why associated:** Revenue, hedging, and investment decisions are all
  priced off these benchmarks.

### B2. Trading, Hedging & Risk Management

- **Sub-domains:** physical vs paper trading; futures/forwards/options;
  collars & zero-cost structures; quotational-period (QP) risk;
  provisional pricing; FX exposure; counterparty & credit risk;
  treasury overlays; gold loans / streaming / royalty financing.
- **Standards / bodies:** LME/CME/COMEX rulebooks; ISDA master
  agreements; IFRS 9 hedge accounting.
- **Source:** LME hedging
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Metal_Exchange **[search-cited]**
- **Why associated:** A mining estate's earnings volatility is managed
  here; the OS must model exposure and propose/track hedges.

### B3. Off-take, Marketing & Sales

- **Sub-domains:** off-take agreement structuring; concentrate sales
  (TC/RCs — treatment & refining charges, payables, penalty elements);
  doré sales; quotational periods; logistics & assay disputes;
  marketing to smelters/traders/refiners; provisional/final invoicing;
  customer due diligence.
- **Standards / bodies:** LME/LBMA settlement conventions; industry
  concentrate contract norms; OECD due diligence on buyers.
- **Source:** LME role as terminal market
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Metal_Exchange **[search-cited]**
- **Why associated:** Converts product into cash; payable-metal terms
  directly drive net revenue per tonne.

### B4. Royalty, Fiscal Regime & Mining Taxation

- **Sub-domains:** ad-valorem & profit-based royalties; clearing/
  inspection fees; corporate income tax; ring-fencing; capital
  allowances & depreciation; withholding tax; VAT/import duty; transfer
  pricing; free-carried & state participation; stabilisation; resource
  rent / windfall taxes; EITI revenue transparency.
- **Standards / bodies:** **Tanzania Mining Act 2010 (as amended 2017)**
  — royalty 6% on gold/copper/silver/platinum + 1% clearing fee;
  **EITI Standard** (Tanzania moderate 77/100, next validation Jan
  2026); IMF/IGF fiscal frameworks; OECD transfer pricing.
- **Source:** Tanzania Mining Act 2010
  https://www.madini.go.tz/media/The-Mining-Act-2010.pdf
  **[search-cited]**; EITI Tanzania https://eiti.org/countries/tanzania
  **[search-cited]**; NRGI briefing
  https://resourcegovernance.org/sites/default/files/documents/tanzania-new-natural-legislation-what-will-change.pdf
  **[search-cited]**
- **Why associated:** The single largest external claim on estate cash
  flow; must be computed, accrued, and reported per jurisdiction.

### B5. Mining Law, Tenure & Licensing

- **Sub-domains:** prospecting/retention/mining/special mining licences;
  primary mining licences (ASM); processing/refining/dealer/broker
  licences; tenure administration & renewals; ground rent; relinquish-
  ment; surface rights vs mineral rights; local content & ownership
  rules; framework/development agreements; state participation;
  beneficiation mandates; dispute resolution; mineral marketplaces &
  trading hubs.
- **Standards / bodies:** **Tanzania Mining Commission (Tume ya
  Madini)**; Mining Act 2010 + Local Content Regulations (≥20% local
  ownership for operators); **EITI**; Ministry of Minerals.
- **Source:** Tume ya Madini regulations
  https://www.tumemadini.go.tz/publications/regulations/
  **[search-cited]**; Local content (Lexology)
  https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=103b9b3c-32df-4ed4-83cc-887b5757a2bc
  **[search-cited]**
- **Why associated:** The estate's right to operate; licence lifecycle &
  compliance obligations are a first-class OS workflow.

### B6. Mineral & Mining Asset Valuation

- **Sub-domains:** income (DCF/NPV/IRR), market (comparable
  transactions), and cost approaches; option/real-options valuation;
  techno-economic modelling; M&A & JV valuation; fairness opinions;
  tax & litigation valuation; impairment testing.
- **Standards / bodies:** **IMVAL** (umbrella) harmonising **VALMIN**
  (Australasia), **SAMVAL** (S. Africa), **CIMVal** (Canada), **SME**
  (USA), and aligning to **IVS / RICS** International Valuation
  Standards.
- **Source:** CIMVAL Code
  https://mrmr.cim.org/media/1147/cimval-code-november2019_may2022.pdf
  **[search-cited]**; IMVAL template (VALMIN)
  https://www.valmin.org/imval_template/ **[search-cited]**
- **Why associated:** Family-office wealth, financing collateral, and
  transaction decisions all rest on defensible asset valuation.

## PART C — OPERATIONS ENABLEMENT

### C1. Treasury & Project Finance

- **Sub-domains:** project finance (non-/limited-recourse, reserve-based
  lending); equity/debt/mezzanine; royalty & streaming finance; export
  credit agencies & DFIs; cash management; working-capital & inventory
  finance; FX/liquidity management; capital allocation & dividend
  policy; stage-gated CAPEX (FEL/PEx scoping→prefeasibility→feasibility).
- **Standards / bodies:** **Equator Principles (EP4)**; **IFC
  Performance Standards (8)**; lender technical/independent engineer
  norms; IFRS for financial instruments.
- **Source:** Equator Principles EP4
  https://equator-principles.com/app/uploads/The-Equator-Principles_EP4_July2020.pdf
  **[search-cited]**; IFC Performance Standards (MINING.COM)
  https://www.mining.com/beyond-the-mine-mouth-the-equator-principles-and-ifc-performance-standards/
  **[search-cited]**
- **Why associated:** Funds the estate's growth; lenders impose ESG/EP
  conditions the OS must continuously evidence.

### C2. Procurement, Supply Chain & Logistics

- **Sub-domains:** strategic sourcing & category management; supplier
  qualification & local-content sourcing; contract management; inventory
  & spares (MRO) optimisation; warehousing; inbound (reagents, fuel,
  explosives, grinding media) & outbound (concentrate/doré) logistics;
  customs/export clearance; cold-chain not applicable but bonded-
  warehouse & ports relevant; supply-chain due diligence.
- **Standards / bodies:** **OECD Due Diligence Guidance for Responsible
  Supply Chains of Minerals** (3T+gold, conflict-affected/high-risk
  areas); ISO 20400 sustainable procurement; Incoterms; local content
  regulations.
- **Source:** OECD Due Diligence Guidance
  https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2013/03/oecd-due-diligence-guidance-for-responsible-supply-chains-of-minerals-from-conflict-affected-and-high-risk-areas_g1g238a3/9789264185050-en.pdf
  **[search-cited]**; OECD 2026 essentials
  https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/07/responsible-business-conduct-spotlights_f7f722d0/due-diligence-essentials-for-responsible-minerals_d3c5ff56/0e12aaca-en.pdf
  **[search-cited]**
- **Why associated:** Cost, availability, and ethical-sourcing risk all
  flow through procurement; local content is a legal obligation.

### C3. Machinery, Equipment, Maintenance & Reliability

- **Sub-domains:** fleet & fixed-plant asset register; preventive/
  predictive/condition-based maintenance; **RCM**; failure-mode
  taxonomy & FMEA; reliability & availability (MTBF/MTTR); spares
  strategy; OEM vs MARC (maintenance-and-repair contracts); CMMS/EAM;
  condition monitoring (vibration, oil analysis, thermography);
  asset-life & replacement modelling; capital-spares.
- **Standards / bodies:** **ISO 14224** (reliability & maintenance data
  taxonomy); **ISO 55000/55001** (asset management); **SAE JA1011/
  JA1012** (RCM); IEC 60300 (dependability).
- **Source:** ISO 14224 vs other standards (NRX)
  https://www.nrx.com/iso-14224-vs-other-standards/ **[search-cited]**;
  RCM guide (Optimal)
  https://optimal.world/reliability-centred-maintenance-guide-evolution-towards-asset-reliability-as-a-service-araas-2/
  **[search-cited]**
- **Why associated:** Equipment availability dictates production; this is
  Borjie's machinery-advisory + maintenance-taxonomy junior domain.

### C4. Construction & Civil Engineering (Mine Infrastructure)

- **Sub-domains:** EPCM project delivery; earthworks & site
  establishment; process-plant civil/structural/mechanical; **tailings
  storage facility (TSF) design & construction** (geotechnical, dam
  engineering, dry-stack/filtered tailings); water dams & ponds; haul
  roads; power & water reticulation; workshops; **mine camps & worker
  housing**; ports/rail spurs; conveyors.
- **Standards / bodies:** **GISTM** (tailings construction governance);
  geotechnical & dam-safety codes (ANCOLD/CDA/ICOLD); Eurocodes;
  national building codes; FIDIC contracts; ISO 19650 (BIM).
- **Source:** Knight Piésold water/dam engineering
  https://knightpiesold.com/en/news/publications/water-engineering-for-modern-mining-bridging-mining-with-sustainability/
  **[search-cited]**; dam engineering advances (MDPI)
  https://www.mdpi.com/2412-3811/10/12/331 **[search-cited]**
- **Why associated:** Builds the physical estate; TSF failure is the
  industry's catastrophic tail risk (GISTM mandate).

### C5. Real Estate, Housing & Camp Estate Management

- **Sub-domains:** land acquisition & access agreements; resettlement
  action plans (RAP); workforce accommodation (camps, FIFO/DIDO);
  facilities management; staff housing & townships; catering & camp
  services; land-tenure register; surface-rights compensation; estate
  & ancillary-property holdings.
- **Standards / bodies:** **IFC PS5** (Land Acquisition & Involuntary
  Resettlement); RICS property management; national land law.
- **Source:** IFC Performance Standards
  https://theenvironmentconsultant.com/2025/03/13/ifc-performance-standards/
  **[search-cited]**
- **Why associated:** The estate is also a property portfolio; housing,
  camps, and land compensation are managed assets and social-licence
  factors.

## PART D — PEOPLE, SAFETY & GOVERNANCE

### D1. Health, Safety & Environment (HSE)

- **Sub-domains:** occupational health & hygiene (dust/silica, noise,

  Borjie's safety/HSE junior owns CCM and incident workflows.

  https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0301420717302623

### E2. Insurance & Risk Transfer
